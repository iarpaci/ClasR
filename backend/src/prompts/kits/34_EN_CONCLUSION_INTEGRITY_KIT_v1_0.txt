## 1. PURPOSE (ONE SENTENCE)

To surface whether the claims made in the conclusion
are traceable to the results and discussion —
mapping new claim introduction, contribution inflation,
and result-conclusion disproportionality as structural
integrity signals, without producing evaluation.

---

## 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Argument Chain Kit tracks the central claim longitudinally
across four transitions. Discussion Scope Drift Kit reads
the Results → Discussion directional distance.

Neither reads the conclusion zone specifically.

The conclusion is a structurally distinct zone:
it is the manuscript's final public claim — the statement
that remains in the reader's memory, that gets cited,
that shapes how the manuscript is positioned in a field.

When the conclusion introduces claims not traceable
to the results, or inflates contributions beyond
what the analysis supports, this is a structural
integrity signal regardless of what the rest
of the manuscript does correctly.

```
Results zone signals extracted
         ↓
Discussion zone signals extracted
         ↓
Conclusion zone signals extracted
         ↓
Traceability map assembled:
  each conclusion claim → results source
         ↓
New introductions and inflations identified
         ↓
Integrity profile reported — no verdict
```

---

## 3. CONCLUSION ZONE DEFINITION

The conclusion zone includes:
- Explicitly labeled Conclusion section
- Concluding Remarks or Summary section
- Final paragraphs of Discussion when no separate Conclusion exists
- Abstract conclusion statement (read separately — see § 7)

The conclusion zone does not include:
- Implications sections read as Discussion continuations
- Future work sections (read separately by existing kits)
- Acknowledgments or declarations

---

## 4. SIGNALS READ

### 4.1 New Claim Introduction

A claim appears in the conclusion that was not
present in results or discussion.

Label: CONCLUSION_NEW_CLAIM
Magnitude: [MINOR / MODERATE / MAJOR]

MINOR: single hedged claim extension
MODERATE: clear new claim without results anchor
MAJOR: conclusion reframes the manuscript's
       central finding in a new direction

### 4.2 Contribution Inflation in Conclusion

The conclusion's contribution claim exceeds
what the study's design, scope, or data can support —
specifically in relation to what was delivered
in the results and discussion.

Label: CONCLUSION_CONTRIBUTION_INFLATION
Intensity: [LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH]

### 4.3 Result-Conclusion Disproportionality

The conclusion is significantly denser in claim weight
than the results it summarizes.

Label: RESULT_CONCLUSION_DISPROPORTION
Direction: [CONCLUSION_EXCEEDS / CONCLUSION_UNDERSELLS]

### 4.4 Limitation Reversal

The manuscript acknowledges limitations in SECTION 8
but the conclusion proceeds as if those limitations
do not apply to its claims.

Label: LIMITATION_REVERSAL

### 4.5 Abstract-Conclusion Consistency

The conclusion's central claim compared
to the abstract's conclusion statement.

States:
```
CONSISTENT    → abstract and conclusion carry the same claim
ESCALATED     → conclusion claim stronger than abstract
DEFLATED      → conclusion claim weaker than abstract
SUBSTITUTED   → conclusion introduces a different central claim
               than the abstract declared
```

## 6. Q-VARIANT CALIBRATION

Q1:
  All five signal types scanned at full sensitivity.
  CONCLUSION_NEW_CLAIM at MODERATE or MAJOR explicitly surfaced.
  LIMITATION_REVERSAL noted as potential reviewer concern.
  SUBSTITUTED abstract-conclusion noted as desk-reject signal.

Q2:
  All signals reported at standard detail.
  CONCLUSION_NEW_CLAIM at MAJOR noted.
  CONCLUSION_CONTRIBUTION_INFLATION at HIGH noted.

Q3:
  CONCLUSION_NEW_CLAIM at MAJOR reported.
  RESULT_CONCLUSION_DISPROPORTION reported.
  LIMITATION_REVERSAL reported.
  Minor signals suppressed silently.

## 8. HARD LIMITS

This kit must never:
  Evaluate the scholarly significance of conclusions
  Recommend specific conclusion revisions
  Label conclusions as over-reaching or under-reaching
  Produce a conclusion quality score
  Act as a standalone conclusion review tool

---

## 9. CANONICAL DEFINITION

CONCLUSION INTEGRITY KIT v1.0 surfaces whether the claims
made in the conclusion are traceable to results and discussion —
mapping new claim introduction, contribution inflation,
result-conclusion disproportionality, limitation reversal,
and abstract-conclusion consistency — as structural integrity
signals, without producing judgment, evaluation, or recommendation.
