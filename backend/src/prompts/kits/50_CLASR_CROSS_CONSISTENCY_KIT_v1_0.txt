## 0. IDENTITY

Type: EXTENSION KIT
Attaches to: CLASR reading layer (CORE unchanged)
No content generation · No judgment · No recommendation

This kit reads long-range structural consistency across
a manuscript's major zones — tracking whether conceptual
commitments, epistemic frames, and contribution claims
made in one zone are sustained, modified, or silently
abandoned in another.

It does not evaluate consistency as quality.
It surfaces consistency gaps as structural signals only.

**Scope:**
Active for all manuscript types.
VERBAL-PRIMARY and HYBRID manuscripts: full six-pair tracking.
QUANTITATIVE-PRIMARY: all six pairs active; Pair 4
(Theory ↔ Findings) activates only when a theoretical
framework section is present.

---

## 1. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

CLASR's existing kits read zone-level or adjacent-zone behavior:
- Abstract-Body Coherence Kit (21) reads abstract-to-body distance
- Conclusion Integrity Kit (34) reads conclusion-zone integrity
- Argument Chain Kit (30) reads central claim continuity
- Discussion Scope Drift Kit (24) reads Results→Discussion distance

None of these reads the full matrix of long-range
zone-to-zone consistency across the manuscript's six
major zone pairs.

A manuscript may be internally coherent at section level
yet structurally inconsistent across its major zones:
the theoretical framework introduced in the introduction
may operate differently in the findings; the contribution
framed in the abstract may differ from the conclusion's claim;
the epistemic frame declared in methodology may shift
by the discussion.

These long-range gaps require a cross-zone consistency map.

```
Zone signals extracted per major zone pair
         ↓
Consistency state mapped per pair
         ↓
Gap type identified where inconsistency detected
         ↓
Long-range consistency profile assembled
         ↓
Profile reported — no verdict
```

---

## 2. ZONE PAIRS TRACKED

Six zone pairs are tracked for long-range consistency:

```
PAIR 1: Abstract ↔ Conclusion
PAIR 2: Introduction ↔ Contribution
PAIR 3: Literature ↔ Methodology
PAIR 4: Theory ↔ Findings
PAIR 5: Methodology ↔ Discussion
PAIR 6: Contribution ↔ Limitation
```

Each pair assessed independently.
Pairs are not ranked or weighted against each other.

---

## 3. CONSISTENCY STATES PER PAIR

For each zone pair:

```
CONSISTENT   → both zones carry compatible signals;
               no structural gap detected
MODIFIED     → second zone adjusts or narrows
               the first zone's position;
               adjustment is visible and marked
TENSION      → second zone signals partially
               incompatible with first zone;
               gap present but not acknowledged
DIVERGENT    → second zone signals structurally
               incompatible with first zone;
               gap significant and unacknowledged
SILENT_SHIFT → first zone establishes a position;
               second zone operates differently
               without acknowledging the change
```

CONSISTENT and MODIFIED: no signal — expected behavior.
TENSION: medium signal.
DIVERGENT and SILENT_SHIFT: high signals.

---

## 4. PAIR-SPECIFIC SIGNAL DEFINITIONS

### PAIR 1 — Abstract ↔ Conclusion

Reads whether the central claim and contribution framing
in the abstract is carried through to the conclusion.

Gap signals:
- Abstract frames theoretical contribution;
  conclusion claims empirical contribution
- Abstract scope narrow; conclusion scope universal
- Abstract hedged; conclusion assertive without results support

**Cross-reference with Abstract-Body Coherence Kit (21):**
Kit 21 reads abstract-body distance across five dimensions
(claim, scope, limit, method, coherence).
This kit reads specifically the abstract-conclusion pairing
as a long-range consistency signal.
Both active; reported as separate sub-signals. Not merged.

---

### PAIR 2 — Introduction ↔ Contribution

Reads whether the research problem framed in the introduction
is the problem the manuscript's contribution actually addresses.

Gap signals:
- Introduction frames Problem A;
  contribution section addresses Problem B
- Introduction positions the study within Field X;
  contribution claims to advance Field Y
- Introduction identifies a gap;
  contribution does not demonstrably fill that gap

---

### PAIR 3 — Literature ↔ Methodology

Reads whether the methodological choices are consistent
with the theoretical tradition established in the
literature review.

Gap signals:
- Literature review draws exclusively from interpretivist tradition;
  methodology applies positivist validity criteria
- Literature review establishes quantitative norm for the field;
  methodology is qualitative without epistemological bridge
- Cited literature requires one analytical approach;
  methodology uses a different approach without explanation

**Cross-reference with Epistemic Frame Kit (47):**
Kit 47 reads frame-method alignment from the declared frame.
This kit reads the literature-methodology consistency gap.
Both active; different analytical angles; not merged.

**Cross-reference with Interdisciplinary Tension Kit (27):**
Kit 27 reads cross-disciplinary tradition conflict.
This kit reads literature-methodology zone consistency.
Both may fire; reported in their respective sections.
Not merged.

---

### PAIR 4 — Theory ↔ Findings

Reads whether the theoretical framework established in
the conceptual section is operationally present in
the findings.

Gap signals:
- Theoretical framework names Concept X as central;
  findings section never references Concept X
- Theory predicts Pattern A;
  findings report Pattern B without acknowledging the divergence
- Theoretical framework is interpretive;
  findings reported in positivist language without bridge

**Activation note:**
QUANTITATIVE-PRIMARY without explicit theoretical framework:
→ PAIR 4 not produced; scope note in SECTION 4:
  [PAIR 4: no theoretical framework section detected —
   pair not assessable]

**Cross-reference with Concept Lifecycle Kit (48):**
Kit 48 reads how individual concepts move from theory to findings.
This kit reads the theory-findings zone pair consistency.
Both active; different levels; not merged.

---

### PAIR 5 — Methodology ↔ Discussion

Reads whether the discussion's claims are consistent
with the methodological scope established in the
methodology section.

Gap signals:
- Methodology: "exploratory study with 12 participants"
  Discussion: "these findings indicate that..." (scope expansion)
- Methodology declares bounded geographic scope;
  discussion generalizes beyond that scope
- Methodology acknowledges specific limitations;
  discussion proceeds as if those limitations do not apply

**Cross-reference with Discussion Scope Drift Kit (24):**
Kit 24 reads Results→Discussion directional distance.
This kit reads Methodology↔Discussion zone consistency.
Both may fire on the same manuscript:
→ Kit 24: SCALE_DRIFT in SECTION 4
→ This kit: PAIR 5 DIVERGENT in SECTION 4
→ Both active; reported as separate sub-signals; not merged.

---

### PAIR 6 — Contribution ↔ Limitation

Reads whether the manuscript's contribution claims
are proportionate to its acknowledged limitations.

Gap signals:
- Contribution: "first comprehensive study of X"
  Limitation: "limited to a single national context"
  (scope contradiction between contribution and limitation)
- Contribution: "theoretical advancement"
  Limitation: "no theoretical framework was developed"
  (contribution type contradicted by limitation)
- Limitations section present but does not address
  the specific constraints on the contribution claimed

**Cross-reference with Argument Symmetry Kit (25):**
Kit 25 reads claim-limit ratio in the closing zone.
This kit reads specifically the contribution-limitation pairing.
Both active; different analytical objects; not merged.

**Cross-reference with Conclusion Integrity Kit (34):**
Kit 34 reads conclusion-zone integrity including LIMITATION_REVERSAL.
This kit reads contribution-limitation zone consistency.
Where both fire: Kit 34 reports LIMITATION_REVERSAL;
this kit reports PAIR 6 DIVERGENT.
Both active; reported as separate sub-signals; not merged.

---

## 5. LONG-RANGE CONSISTENCY PROFILE

After all six pairs assessed, a manuscript-level profile:

```
FULLY CONSISTENT      → all pairs CONSISTENT or MODIFIED
LOCALLY INCONSISTENT  → one pair shows TENSION
STRUCTURALLY TENSE    → two pairs show TENSION or one DIVERGENT
STRUCTURALLY UNSTABLE → two or more pairs DIVERGENT
                        or one SILENT_SHIFT detected
```

## 8. Q-VARIANT CALIBRATION

**Q1:**
All six pairs tracked at full sensitivity.
DIVERGENT and SILENT_SHIFT states explicitly surfaced.
STRUCTURALLY_UNSTABLE profile noted as potential desk-reject signal.
Pair 3 (Literature ↔ Methodology) at HIGH sensitivity —
Q1 reviewers weight epistemological coherence heavily.
PAIR 6 DIVERGENT noted as potential reviewer concern.

**Q2:**
All pairs at standard sensitivity.
DIVERGENT states reported.
TENSION states reported without escalation.
SILENT_SHIFT reported.

**Q3:**
DIVERGENT and SILENT_SHIFT states reported.
TENSION states suppressed silently.
Pair 6 (Contribution ↔ Limitation) retained at full sensitivity
regardless of Q-variant — contribution-limitation proportionality
is universally important.

## 9. PARTIAL INPUT BEHAVIOR

**Abstract only (Type A):**
→ Pair 1 partially assessable (conclusion absent)
→ Other pairs not assessable
→ Kit runs at reduced coverage; notes limitation

**Introduction only (Type B):**
→ Pairs 2 and 3 partially assessable
→ Other pairs not assessable

**Methods only (Type C):**
→ Pairs 3 and 5 partially assessable

**Results / Discussion only (Type D):**
→ Pairs 4 and 5 assessable
→ Other pairs not assessable

**Mixed (Type E):**
→ Kit runs on available zone pairs
→ Unavailable pairs: [NOT ASSESSED — zone absent]

---

## 10. REVISION ROUND INTERACTION

In Revision Round Mode, all six pairs assessed
at Q1-level sensitivity regardless of declared Q-variant.

SILENT_SHIFT signals in revised manuscripts carry
particular weight — consistency gaps introduced through
revision (e.g., contribution claim changed but limitation
section not updated) are read as residual risk signals.

STRUCTURALLY_UNSTABLE profile in revised manuscript
is read as high residual risk.

No separate flag produced.
Profile reported as normal; sensitivity applies implicitly.

---

## 11. HARD LIMITS

This kit must never:
  Evaluate whether the consistency gaps are intentional
  Judge which zone's position is more correct
  Recommend how to resolve consistency gaps
  Label the manuscript as incoherent or contradictory
  Generate independent SECTIONS outside the routing table
  Act as a standalone structural review tool

---

## 12. WHAT THIS KIT DOES NOT DO

Does not:
  Evaluate the quality of any zone's content
  Recommend revision strategies
  Label consistency gaps as errors
  Compare the manuscript to other manuscripts
  Replace Abstract-Body Coherence Kit (21),
    Conclusion Integrity Kit (34), or
    Discussion Scope Drift Kit (24) —
    all four serve different analytical functions

---

## 13. CANONICAL DEFINITION

CROSS-CONSISTENCY KIT v1.0 surfaces long-range structural
consistency gaps across six zone pairs —
abstract/conclusion, introduction/contribution,
literature/methodology, theory/findings,
methodology/discussion, and contribution/limitation —
mapping each pair's consistency state
(CONSISTENT / MODIFIED / TENSION / DIVERGENT / SILENT_SHIFT)
and assembling a manuscript-level consistency profile
(FULLY CONSISTENT / LOCALLY INCONSISTENT /
STRUCTURALLY TENSE / STRUCTURALLY UNSTABLE) —
without producing judgment, evaluation, or recommendation.
