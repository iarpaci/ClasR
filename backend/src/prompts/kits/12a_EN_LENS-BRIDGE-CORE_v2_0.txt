---

## 0. IDENTITY

Type: EXTENSION KIT
Attaches to: UNIFIED-OUTPUT KIT v1.1 (before closing block)
No judgment · No recommendation · No rewriting

This kit maps the distribution of claim load
across manuscript sections as a cross-section signal layer.

---

## 1. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Existing lenses read each signal tied to one SECTION.
This kit operates differently:

```
After all SECTION outputs are produced
         ↓
Signal count per SECTION is aggregated
         ↓
Load distribution is mapped
         ↓
Single block displayed before the closing block
```

Does not touch SECTION order.
Does not open new SECTIONs.
Re-reads lens outputs; does not re-produce them.

---

## 2. MEASURED ZONES

The manuscript is evaluated across four functional zones:

```
FRAMING   → SECTION 0 + SECTION 1 + SECTION 2
CORE      → SECTION 3 + SECTION 4 + SECTION 5
LANGUAGE  → SECTION 6
CLOSING   → SECTION 7 + SECTION 8
```

---

## 3. DENSITY MEASUREMENT

For each zone, active signal counts from relevant SECTIONs are aggregated:
- Verbal Lens signals
- LENSSA-DEPTH patterns
- FREQUENCY SIGNAL outputs
- Temporality / Silence / Orientation / Integrity signal detections
- Citation Behavior signals
- Abstract–Body Coherence signals
- Replication Signal profile gaps
- Contribution Framing signals
- Discussion Scope Drift signals
- Argument Symmetry signals
- Negative Result Visibility signals
- Interdisciplinary Tension signals
- Reader Model signals
- Desk-Reject Signal Kit trigger zones (FRAMING zone)
- Argument Chain Kit signals (CORE zone)
- Argument Load Kit signals (CORE zone)
- Concept-Evidence Bridge Kit signals (FRAMING + CORE zones)
- Conclusion Integrity Kit signals (CLOSING zone)
- Overreach-Signal Kit signals (all zones, per routing table)
- Uncertainty-Visibility Kit signals (CLOSING zone)
- Hedging-Calibration Kit signals (LANGUAGE zone)
- Journal-Sensitivity Kit signals (FRAMING + LANGUAGE zones)

Signal count → zone load:
```
0–1 signals  → LOW
2–3 signals  → MEDIUM
4+  signals  → HIGH
```

---

## 4. DISTRIBUTION PROFILES

Four primary distribution patterns recognized:

**FRONT-LOADED:**
FRAMING zone HIGH, CLOSING zone LOW
→ Strong promise; quiet closing

**BACK-LOADED:**
FRAMING zone LOW, CLOSING zone HIGH
→ Modest opening; overstated conclusion

**CORE-CONCENTRATED:**
CORE zone HIGH, others LOW or MEDIUM
→ Load concentrated in method and argumentation

**DISTRIBUTED:**
All zones MEDIUM or balanced
→ Load spread evenly across the manuscript

Multiple profiles may be detected simultaneously.

---

## 5. OUTPUT FORMAT

Displayed immediately before the closing block:

```
▸ ARGUMENT DENSITY

FRAMING:  [LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH]
CORE:     [LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH]
LANGUAGE: [LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH]
CLOSING:  [LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH]

Distribution: [FRONT-LOADED / BACK-LOADED /
               CORE-CONCENTRATED / DISTRIBUTED]
```

Maximum 6 lines.
No interpretation. No recommendation.
Distribution only.

---

## 6. ACTIVATION

Active in every report automatically.
If signal count is zero — all zones reported as LOW.
User is not separately notified.

---

## 7. HARD LOCK

This kit:
  Does not alter SECTION content
  Does not generate new signals
  Does not produce "weak/strong" judgments
  Only maps distribution
  Does not break UNIFIED-OUTPUT format

---

## 8. CANONICAL DEFINITION

ARGUMENT-DENSITY v1.0 aggregates signal load from all SECTION outputs
by functional zone and maps how claim weight is distributed
across the manuscript, without producing interpretation or judgment.

## 0. IDENTITY

This file defines which overlay lens connects to which SECTION
and how each lens integrates within the CLASR-EN output.

Companion file 12b_EN_LENS-BRIDGE-COLLISION_v2_0.txt
defines collision management rules when multiple lenses
detect the same signal.

Both files must be present for full Lens-Bridge operation.

---

## 1. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Lens overlays do not operate independently.
Every lens is bound to a specific SECTION's sub-layer.

```
SECTION production
        ↓
Is a lens bound to this SECTION?
    Yes → Lens runs signal scan
           Signal found → label appears + integrated into SECTION output
           No signal    → lens runs silently
    No  → Lens inactive for this SECTION
```

Lens output never opens a new section.
Always appears within the relevant SECTION as a sub-signal.

**Label enforcement:** Every detected signal produces a visible label.
Q-variant and context length do not suppress labels.
See UNIFIED-OUTPUT KIT v1.1 § 6 for full label enforcement rules.

### Journal-Sensitivity Kit (JOURNAL_SENSITIVITY_KIT v1.0) — NEW v2.0
**Primary:** SECTION 0 — Macro Frame
  Field detection result and calibration status
  → field profile sub-signal in SECTION 0
  → positioned after AUTO-Q estimate
**Secondary:** SECTION 2 — Conceptual / Theoretical Framework
  Field-specific terminology signals
  → field term sub-signal in SECTION 2
**Tertiary:** SECTION 6 — Academic Language & Hedging
  Field-specific frequency and hedging calibration adjustments
  → field calibration note in SECTION 6
**Activation:** Automatically active for every report.
  Field detection attempted from manuscript signals.
  User override accepted at any point.
  If field cannot be determined — FIELD-INT (default) applied.
**Threshold behavior:**
  Field calibration applies before kit signal production.
  Q-Gate minimum always preserved; MAJOR ceiling never exceeded.
  Field calibration cannot suppress a signal below Q-Gate minimum.
  Field calibration cannot amplify above MAJOR severity.
**Relationship to Q-Gate:**
  Q-Gate governs tier sensitivity.
  Journal-Sensitivity Kit governs field sensitivity.
  Both active simultaneously; not merged.
