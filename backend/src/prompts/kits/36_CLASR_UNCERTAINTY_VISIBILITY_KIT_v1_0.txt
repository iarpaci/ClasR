# 36 CLASR — UNCERTAINTY-VISIBILITY KIT v1.0
# Kit 36 — Uncertainty Positioning and Visibility Profile
# CLASR Tier 3 Extension
# Compatible with: CLASR v2.1

---

## 0. IDENTITY

This kit reads how uncertainty is positioned within the manuscript —
not whether it is present, but how visible, how early, how consistently,
and how structurally it is handled.

This kit:
  Does not produce verdicts or evaluations
  Does not recommend revision
  Does not suppress or modify manuscript content
  Does not replace SECTION 8 (it extends it)

---

## 1. DIVISION OF RESPONSIBILITY

### With Calibration-Deep §1.3:

Calibration-Deep §1.3 reads:
  Is uncertainty present?
  Is SECTION 8 absent or under 3 lines?
  → Triggers mandatory SECTION 8 production if threshold crossed.
  This is a presence detection and correction behavior.

Uncertainty-Visibility Kit reads:
  How is uncertainty positioned?
  Where does it appear relative to claims?
  How consistently is it distributed?
  Is it structurally embedded or end-appended?
  This is a visibility and positioning behavior, not a correction behavior.

Co-occurrence rule:
  Both kits are active simultaneously.
  They are not merged in output.
  Calibration-Deep correction runs first if §1.3 threshold is crossed.
  Uncertainty-Visibility Kit runs on all reports regardless.

### With Hedging-Calibration Kit (SECTION 6 overlap):
  Hedging-Calibration Kit reads hedging consistency across claim types.
  Uncertainty-Visibility Kit reads where uncertainty is positioned
  relative to the argument structure.
  Both active; reported as separate sub-signals.
  Hedging-Calibration in SECTION 6; Uncertainty-Visibility in SECTION 8.

---

## 2. VISIBILITY DIMENSIONS (5)

Uncertainty-Visibility Kit reads along five dimensions.

### Dimension 1 — POSITION

Where does uncertainty acknowledgment appear
relative to the claims it qualifies?

```
PROXIMATE   — uncertainty appears immediately after or alongside the claim
DEFERRED    — uncertainty appears in a later section, separated from the claim
TERMINAL    — uncertainty appears only in SECTION 8 / Limitations
ABSENT      — no uncertainty acknowledgment found for a bounded claim
```

PROXIMATE is the strongest positioning.
TERMINAL is not a failure — it is the minimum standard.
ABSENT triggers Calibration-Deep §1.3 simultaneously.

---

### Dimension 2 — DISTRIBUTION

How is uncertainty acknowledgment distributed across the manuscript?

```
DISTRIBUTED   — uncertainty appears across multiple sections
                (Methods, Results, Discussion, Limitations)
CONCENTRATED  — uncertainty appears only in one section (typically Limitations)
FRONT-LOADED  — uncertainty acknowledged in Abstract or Introduction
                as well as body sections
ABSENT        — no distribution pattern detectable
```

---

### Dimension 3 — SCOPE MATCH

Does the scope of uncertainty acknowledgment
match the scope of the claims being made?

```
MATCHED      — uncertainty acknowledgment covers the primary claims
PARTIAL      — uncertainty acknowledged for some claims, not others
ASYMMETRIC   — major claims left without uncertainty acknowledgment;
               minor or secondary claims acknowledged
ABSENT       — no match detectable
```

---

### Dimension 4 — STRUCTURAL INTEGRATION

Is uncertainty embedded in the argument structure
or appended as a formal section only?

```
INTEGRATED   — uncertainty is woven into the presentation of findings;
               limitations acknowledged within argumentation
APPENDED     — uncertainty appears only as a separate Limitations section;
               not referenced within findings or discussion
PERFORMATIVE — limitations section present but does not connect
               to the claims made in the manuscript
```

APPENDED is common and not inherently problematic.
PERFORMATIVE is the signal of concern.

---

### Dimension 5 — LANGUAGE REGISTER

What language register is used to signal uncertainty?

```
EPISTEMIC    — hedging language marks claims as contingent
               ("suggests", "may indicate", "is consistent with")
ASSERTIVE    — claims are stated without hedging markers
               despite bounded evidence
MIXED        — epistemic register used inconsistently;
               some claims hedged, others not
```

---

## 3. VISIBILITY PROFILE ASSEMBLY

After reading all five dimensions,
a composite visibility profile is assigned.

### TRANSPARENT
  Uncertainty is proximate or front-loaded,
  distributed across sections,
  scope-matched to primary claims,
  structurally integrated,
  and consistently expressed in epistemic register.

### PARTIAL
  Uncertainty is acknowledged but concentrated or terminal,
  scope-match is partial or asymmetric,
  language register is mixed.
  Acknowledgment is present but not fully visible
  at the claim level.

### APPENDED
  Uncertainty appears only in a Limitations section.
  Not distributed, not proximate to claims,
  not integrated into argument structure.
  Standard but minimal positioning.

### SUPPRESSED
  One or more primary claims carry no uncertainty acknowledgment.
  Scope match is absent or severely asymmetric.
  Language register is assertive for bounded claims.
  Calibration-Deep §1.3 is co-active when SECTION 8 is absent.

Primary section: SECTION 8 — Limits & Uncertainties

```

```

---

## 5. ACTIVATION

Automatically active in SECTION 8 for every report.
No user command required.
If visibility profile is TRANSPARENT → single line only:

```
UNCERTAINTY VISIBILITY PROFILE: TRANSPARENT
```

If any dimension is PARTIAL, APPENDED, ASYMMETRIC, SUPPRESSED,
ASSERTIVE, or MIXED → full structured block produced.

---

## 6. RELATIONSHIP TO SECTION 8 MANDATORY REQUIREMENT

SECTION 8 is mandatory in every full report (Calibration-Deep §1.3).
This kit extends SECTION 8 — it does not replace it.
If SECTION 8 is absent, Calibration-Deep §1.3 triggers first.
Uncertainty-Visibility Kit then runs on the produced SECTION 8.

---

## 7. HARD LOCKS

This kit must never:
  Replace or suppress SECTION 8
  Produce a verdict on the manuscript's overall uncertainty handling
  Recommend revision or correction actions
  Merge its output with Calibration-Deep §1.3 output
  Assign TRANSPARENT when any dimension reads below that level
  Assign SUPPRESSED without evidence of absent acknowledgment
    for a primary claim

---

## 8. CANONICAL DEFINITION

UNCERTAINTY-VISIBILITY-KIT v1.0 reads uncertainty positioning
across five dimensions (POSITION, DISTRIBUTION, SCOPE MATCH,
STRUCTURAL INTEGRATION, LANGUAGE REGISTER),
assigns a composite visibility profile
(TRANSPARENT / PARTIAL / APPENDED / SUPPRESSED),
and produces a structured block in SECTION 8 —
with secondary signals in SECTION 4 and SECTION 6
when asymmetric or assertive patterns are detected —
without correcting manuscript language, producing verdicts,
or replacing SECTION 8's mandatory function.
