## 6. VERSION FREEZE DOCUMENT v2.2

### CHANGE LOG — v2.1 → v2.2

Added:

39_EN_METHODOLOGICAL_RHETORIC_KIT_v1_0
  Surfaces four field-independent methodological rhetoric patterns:
  REPRESENTATIVENESS_TRANSFER — assumption that a measured subset
    fully represents a broader domain, treated as established
    rather than argued; operates as a silent method-layer premise.
  VISUAL_CHECK_AS_VALIDATION — a figure used as evidence
    of methodological validity in place of a formal
    validation procedure.
  DISTRIBUTIONAL_CONFIRMATION_BIAS — a distributional output
    interpreted with hedged language that nonetheless performs
    a confirmatory function the evidence cannot sustain.
  COMPLEMENTARITY_REFRAMING — inter-method disagreement
    dissolved through "complementarity" framing without
    quantitative or theoretical justification.
  Tier 3. Primary: SECTION 3. Secondary: SECTION 4.
  Pattern density: SINGLE / COMPOUND / SYSTEMATIC.
  SYSTEMATIC feeds into Desk-Reject STRUCTURAL_COMPLETENESS_RISK.
  Coordinates with: Calibration-Deep §1.3, Overreach Signal Kit (35),
  Concept-Evidence Bridge Kit (33), Hedging Calibration Kit (37),
  Discussion Scope Drift Kit (24), Argument Symmetry Kit (25),
  Desk-Reject Signal Kit (29), Journal-Sensitivity Kit (38).

### RATIONALE

Gap 8 — Methodological rhetoric layer:
  Existing kits read what claims are made and how they are bounded.
  No kit read the specific rhetorical moves by which
  methodological choices are presented as self-validating,
  visually confirmed, or mutually complementary in ways
  that suppress legitimate methodological uncertainty.
  These patterns are field-independent — they appear across
  quantitative disciplines wherever methods carry argumentative
  weight beyond what they structurally deliver.
  They were first identified through live manuscript scanning
  (CLASR-EN Term Scanner candidate set, May 2026).
  → Methodological Rhetoric Kit v1.0.
