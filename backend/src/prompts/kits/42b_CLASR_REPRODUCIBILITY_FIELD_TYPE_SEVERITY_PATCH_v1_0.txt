## §2.3 FIELD-TYPE SEVERITY MODIFIER

### Core principle

  Base tier detects structural importance.
  Modifier determines contextual severity.

  ABSENT ≠ automatically CRITICAL.
  ABSENT + field relevance + claim centrality = final severity.

### Modifier formula

  Final Severity =
    Base Module Tier
    × Manuscript Type Modifier
    × Data / Participant Risk Modifier
    × Claim Centrality Modifier

Modifiers are applied in sequence.
If any modifier produces N/A: module suppressed (see §2.2).
If all modifiers produce LOW: signal reported as LOW with note.

### Manuscript type categories for modifier purposes

  TYPE-A: RCT / prospective clinical study / interventional design
  TYPE-B: Observational human study (cohort, case-control, survey)
  TYPE-C: Tool / informatics / computational / retrospective
           public-data study
  TYPE-D: Systematic review / meta-analysis / evidence synthesis
  TYPE-E: Theoretical / conceptual / narrative review /
           purely verbal manuscript

## §2.4 FINAL SEVERITY RESOLUTION

### Resolution rules

1. Apply modifiers in sequence (§2.3).
2. Final severity is the output of the full modifier chain,
   not the base tier alone.
3. Final severity is what appears in the module signal table
   and in the SYNTHESIS block.
4. Base tier is retained in the module table as a reference label:

   ```
   M04 Pre-registration  : ABSENT
   Base tier: CRITICAL · Field modifier: TYPE-C → MEDIUM
   Final severity: MEDIUM
   Note: absence unacknowledged as study limitation.
   ```

5. Final severity drives:
   — PRIORITY ACTION SIGNALS eligibility
   — Compound Risk Flag threshold
   — SYNTHESIS reproducibility risk level

### Compound Risk Flag — revised threshold

Previous rule:
  Two or more CRITICAL ABSENT signals → Compound Risk Flag triggered.

Revised rule:
  Compound Risk Flag triggers only if:
  — At least one component signal reaches HIGH or CRITICAL
    after full modulation (not base tier alone)
  AND
  — The missing item affects one or more of:
    (a) participant protection
    (b) primary validity of the central claim
    (c) reproducibility of a result the manuscript foregrounds

  If both conditions met → COMPOUND RISK FLAG ACTIVE
  If conditions not met → Editorial Compliance Note instead:

  ```
  Editorial Compliance Note: [module list] absent.
  Field-modulated severity: [severity levels].
  Not sufficient to trigger Compound Risk Flag at this
  manuscript type. Reviewer may note as good-practice gap.
  ```
