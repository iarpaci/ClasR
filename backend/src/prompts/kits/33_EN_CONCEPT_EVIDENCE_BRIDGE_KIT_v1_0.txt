## 1. PURPOSE (ONE SENTENCE)

To surface whether key concepts in the manuscript
are grounded in evidence — mapping each concept's
operationalization status as GROUNDED, PARTIAL, or FLOATING —
without producing evaluation of the concepts themselves.

---

## 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

CLASR-EN reads how claims are made and bounded.
Contribution Framing Kit reads how novelty is asserted.
This kit reads a different gap:

A manuscript may carry claims that are formally bounded
and nominally supported — yet rest on concepts that are
never operationalized, never defined in measurable terms,
and never connected to the evidence base.

These concepts float above the argument.
They appear to carry weight. They carry none.

```
Key concepts extracted from framing zone
         ↓
Operationalization behavior tracked per concept
         ↓
Evidence connection mapped:
  direct / indirect / absent
         ↓
Grounding profile assembled per concept
         ↓
Floating concepts identified
         ↓
Profile reported — no verdict
```

---

## 3. KEY CONCEPT DETECTION

Key concepts are identified from:
- Title vocabulary
- Abstract claim-carrying terms
- Introduction's definitional zone
- Theoretical framework's central terms
- Terms that recur with HIGH frequency (Frequency Signal Kit)

A concept qualifies as "key" when:
- It appears in 3+ sections of the manuscript
- OR it carries a claim in the abstract or conclusion
- OR it is presented as a contribution marker

Proper nouns, field names, and method names
are excluded from key concept tracking.

---

## 4. OPERATIONALIZATION STATES

For each key concept, operationalization is assessed:

```
DEFINED       → concept is explicitly defined in the manuscript
OPERATIONALIZED → concept is connected to a measurable or
                  observable indicator
ASSUMED       → concept is used as if its meaning is
                shared and self-evident
CONTESTED     → manuscript acknowledges the concept
                is debated or unstable
IMPORTED      → concept is borrowed from another field
                without boundary redefinition
```

---

## 5. EVIDENCE CONNECTION STATES

For each key concept, evidence connection is assessed:

```
DIRECT        → concept is directly supported by data,
                citation, or demonstrated observation
                in this manuscript
INDIRECT      → concept is supported by cited literature
                that the manuscript does not independently verify
ABSENT        → concept carries claims without
                identifiable evidential support
```

---

## 6. GROUNDING PROFILE

Combining operationalization and evidence connection
produces a concept-level grounding status:

```
GROUNDED      → OPERATIONALIZED + DIRECT or INDIRECT support
PARTIAL       → DEFINED or ASSUMED + INDIRECT support
               OR OPERATIONALIZED + ABSENT support
FLOATING      → ASSUMED or IMPORTED + ABSENT support
               concept carries argumentative weight
               with no evidential or definitional anchor
```

FLOATING is the high-signal state.
PARTIAL is the medium-signal state.
GROUNDED produces no signal — expected behavior.

---

## 7. FLOATING CONCEPT PATTERNS

### 7.1 Decorative Abstraction
A high-register concept used to frame the manuscript
but never connected to the analysis.

Label: DECORATIVE_ABSTRACTION

### 7.2 Claim-Carrying Float
A concept is used to make a claim in the conclusion
but was never grounded in the body.

Label: CLAIM_CARRYING_FLOAT

### 7.3 Borrowed Authority
A concept is imported from a high-prestige theoretical
tradition and used to elevate the manuscript's claims
without engaging the tradition's internal debates.

Label: BORROWED_AUTHORITY_CONCEPT

### 7.4 Operationalization Gap
A concept is defined but never connected
to the actual analytical operations of the study.

Label: OPERATIONALIZATION_GAP

---

## 8. OUTPUT BEHAVIOR

Reported as a sub-signal within SECTION 2 and SECTION 4:

```
CONCEPT-EVIDENCE BRIDGE SIGNAL

Concept: [term]
Operationalization: [DEFINED / OPERATIONALIZED /
                     ASSUMED / CONTESTED / IMPORTED]
Evidence connection: [DIRECT / INDIRECT / ABSENT]
Grounding status: [GROUNDED / PARTIAL / FLOATING]
Pattern: [DECORATIVE_ABSTRACTION / CLAIM_CARRYING_FLOAT /
          BORROWED_AUTHORITY_CONCEPT /
          OPERATIONALIZATION_GAP / none]
```

Multiple concepts may be reported.
Each reported separately.

If all key concepts are GROUNDED:
→ Kit runs silently. No note produced.

---

## 9. Q-VARIANT CALIBRATION

Q1:
  FLOATING concepts explicitly surfaced.
  CLAIM_CARRYING_FLOAT noted as potential desk-reject signal.
  BORROWED_AUTHORITY_CONCEPT flagged at HIGH sensitivity.

Q2:
  FLOATING concepts reported at standard detail.
  PARTIAL grounding noted without escalation.
  BORROWED_AUTHORITY_CONCEPT noted if claim-carrying.

Q3:
  FLOATING concepts reported at summary level.
  PARTIAL grounding suppressed silently.
  DECORATIVE_ABSTRACTION noted only if claim-carrying.

---

## 10. INTEGRATION — LENS-BRIDGE CONNECTION

### Primary: SECTION 2 — Conceptual / Theoretical Framework
Key concept operationalization and grounding status
→ reported as concept-bridge sub-signal within SECTION 2

### Secondary: SECTION 4 — Argumentation
Where FLOATING concepts function as argumentative bridges
→ flagged as concept-bridge gap within SECTION 4

### Secondary: SECTION 5 — Numerical / Spatial Behavior
Where OPERATIONALIZATION_GAP exists between
a theoretically defined concept and the numerical indicators
→ noted as concept-measurement alignment signal within SECTION 5

---

## 11. HARD LIMITS

This kit must never:
  Evaluate conceptual validity or theoretical depth
  Recommend specific operationalizations
  Label concepts as appropriate or inappropriate
  Produce a concept quality score
  Act as a standalone theoretical review tool

---

## 12. CANONICAL DEFINITION

CONCEPT-EVIDENCE BRIDGE KIT v1.0 surfaces whether key concepts
in the manuscript are grounded in evidence — mapping each concept's
operationalization status and evidence connection as GROUNDED,
PARTIAL, or FLOATING — and detecting decorative abstraction,
claim-carrying float, borrowed authority, and operationalization gaps —
as structural grounding signals, without producing judgment,
evaluation, or recommendation.
