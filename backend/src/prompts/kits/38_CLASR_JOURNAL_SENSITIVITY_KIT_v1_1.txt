## 1. PURPOSE (ONE SENTENCE)

To calibrate CLASR signal sensitivity to field-specific
publication norms, terminology conventions, and methodological
expectations — mapping where the same signal carries different
weight in different academic fields — without modifying
core signal taxonomy or Q-Gate thresholds.

---

## 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Q-Gate calibrates reading sensitivity to journal tier:
Q1 / Q2 / Q3 — how rigorous is the target journal.

This kit calibrates reading sensitivity to journal field:
History / Medicine / Law / Natural Sciences / Humanities — 
what does rigorous mean in this specific domain.

The same signal can carry different weight depending on field:

  "significant" in statistics → FREQUENCY SIGNAL HIGH risk
  "significant" in history → standard academic qualifier
  
  No limitations section in chemistry → LOW concern
  No limitations section in clinical trials → HIGH concern
  
  Single-author study in humanities → expected norm
  Single-author study in clinical medicine → unusual signal

Q-Gate governs tier sensitivity.
This kit governs field sensitivity.
Both active simultaneously. Not merged.

```
Q-Gate determines: how strict is the reading
         ↓
Journal-Sensitivity Kit determines: what is normal for this field
         ↓
Signal thresholds adjusted per field expectation
         ↓
Field-adjusted profile reported in SECTION 0
```

---

## 3. FIELD TAXONOMY

Eight broad field categories are defined.
Each carries a default signal calibration profile.

```
FIELD-NAT   → Natural Sciences
              (biology, chemistry, physics, earth sciences)

FIELD-MED   → Medical and Health Sciences
              (clinical, epidemiology, public health, pharmacology)

FIELD-SOC   → Social Sciences
              (sociology, political science, economics, psychology)

FIELD-HUM   → Humanities
              (history, literature, philosophy, cultural studies)

FIELD-LAW   → Legal and Regulatory
              (law, compliance, policy analysis)

FIELD-ENG   → Engineering and Applied Sciences
              (computer science, materials, environmental engineering)

FIELD-EDU   → Education Sciences
              (pedagogy, curriculum, learning sciences)

FIELD-INT   → Interdisciplinary
              (mixed field signals; no single field dominant)
```

Field is detected from:
- Journal name or ISSN (if provided by user)
- Manuscript vocabulary and citation patterns
- Method names and statistical approaches
- AUTO-Q estimate field signals

If field cannot be determined:
→ FIELD-INT applied as default
→ Noted in SECTION 0

---

## 4. FIELD-SPECIFIC SIGNAL CALIBRATION

For each field, signal thresholds are adjusted.
Adjustments are applied as modifiers to existing kit signals —
they do not replace or override Q-Gate thresholds.

### 4.1 FIELD-NAT — Natural Sciences

**Reproducibility & Open Science Kit (42) adjustments:**
  Pre-registration: LOW concern (not field norm)
  Data availability: MEDIUM concern (field-dependent)
  Code availability: HIGH concern for computational studies
  Reporting standard: MEDIUM concern

**Uncertainty-Visibility Kit adjustments:**
  No dedicated limitations section: LOW concern
    (limitations typically embedded in discussion)
  Generic uncertainty language: MEDIUM concern

**Frequency Signal Kit adjustments:**
  "significant" in statistical context: standard — not flagged
  "significant" in scope claim context: flagged as normal

**Hedging-Calibration Kit adjustments:**
  EMPIRICAL claims: moderate hedging expected
  CAUSAL claims: high hedging expected unless experimental design

**Contribution framing:**
  Incremental contribution: field norm — LOW signal
  "First study" claims: moderate scrutiny

---

### 4.2 FIELD-MED — Medical and Health Sciences

**Reproducibility & Open Science Kit (42) adjustments:**
  Pre-registration: HIGH concern — clinical norm
  Data availability: HIGH concern
  Reporting standard (CONSORT/STROBE): HIGH concern
  Ethics declaration: MANDATORY — absence is HIGH signal

**Uncertainty-Visibility Kit adjustments:**
  No limitations section: HIGH concern
  Generic limitation language: HIGH concern
  CONTRADICTED limitation: CRITICAL concern

**Overreach-Signal Kit adjustments:**
  TRANSLATIONAL_OVERREACH: maximum sensitivity
    (in vitro → clinical claim: always MAJOR)
  POLICY_OVERREACH: HIGH sensitivity
    (clinical study → treatment recommendation: HIGH)

**Frequency Signal Kit adjustments:**
  "significant" in statistical context: standard
  "significant" in clinical context without effect size: MEDIUM signal
  "risk" without measurement: HIGH signal

**Hedging-Calibration Kit adjustments:**
  All claim types: maximum hedging sensitivity
  CAUSAL claims without RCT design: MAJOR calibration failure

**Field-specific signals:**
  Absolute risk vs. relative risk distinction:
    Relative risk only, no absolute figures → MEDIUM signal
    Label: RISK_FRAMING_SIGNAL
  Number needed to treat absent from clinical claims:
    Label: NNT_ABSENT_SIGNAL
  P-value only, no confidence intervals:
    Label: CI_ABSENT_SIGNAL

---

### 4.3 FIELD-SOC — Social Sciences

**Reproducibility & Open Science Kit (42) adjustments:**
  Pre-registration: MEDIUM concern (growing norm)
  Data availability: MEDIUM concern (field-variable)
  Code availability: MEDIUM concern for quantitative studies

**Uncertainty-Visibility Kit adjustments:**
  No limitations section: MEDIUM concern
  Generic limitation language: MEDIUM concern

**Overreach-Signal Kit adjustments:**
  GENERALIZATION_OVERREACH: HIGH sensitivity
    (single-country study → universal claim: MAJOR)
  CAUSAL_OVERREACH: HIGH sensitivity
    (observational → causal: MAJOR)

**Interdisciplinary Tension Kit adjustments:**
  Mixed quantitative-qualitative without epistemological bridge:
    HIGH sensitivity (field norm requires explicit bridging)

**Field-specific signals:**
  Survey response rate not reported: MEDIUM signal
    Label: RESPONSE_RATE_ABSENT
  Sampling frame not described: MEDIUM signal
    Label: SAMPLING_FRAME_ABSENT

---

### 4.4 FIELD-HUM — Humanities

**Reproducibility & Open Science Kit (42) adjustments:**
  All dimensions: NOT APPLICABLE (field norm)
  No signal produced for absent replication elements

**Uncertainty-Visibility Kit adjustments:**
  No formal limitations section: LOW concern (field norm)
  Generic uncertainty: LOW concern
  Maximum sensitivity suppressed — not field norm

**Frequency Signal Kit adjustments:**
  "significant" as interpretive qualifier: standard — not flagged
  "demonstrates" in argument context: standard — lower threshold
  Normative language: MEDIUM concern (not LOW)
    (humanities has normative tradition — but unmarked normativity is still notable)

**Hedging-Calibration Kit adjustments:**
  EMPIRICAL claims: LOW sensitivity (field uses different evidentiary norms)
  CAUSAL claims: MEDIUM sensitivity
  CONTRIBUTORY claims: standard sensitivity

**Argument Load Kit adjustments:**
  FRONT-LOADED profile: field norm for argumentative humanities
  METHODS-HEAVY: unusual — MEDIUM signal

**Contribution framing:**
  "This reading proposes..." : field norm — LOW signal
  "This study is the first to..." without literature review: HIGH signal

**Field-specific signals:**
  Primary source citation absent for historical claims:
    Label: PRIMARY_SOURCE_ABSENT
    Intensity: [MEDIUM / HIGH] depending on claim weight
  Archival basis not described for historical argument:
    Label: ARCHIVAL_BASIS_ABSENT

---

### 4.5 FIELD-LAW — Legal and Regulatory

**Reproducibility & Open Science Kit (42) adjustments:**
  All dimensions: NOT APPLICABLE (field norm)

**Uncertainty-Visibility Kit adjustments:**
  No limitations section: LOW concern (field norm)
  Normative language: LOW concern
    (law is inherently normative — unmarked normativity is field standard)

**Overreach-Signal Kit adjustments:**
  POLICY_OVERREACH: LOW sensitivity
    (policy prescription is field norm in legal scholarship)
  CAUSAL_OVERREACH: MEDIUM sensitivity

**Frequency Signal Kit adjustments:**
  "should" / "must" in legal analysis: LOW concern (field norm)
  "should" in empirical claim context: MEDIUM concern

**Contribution framing:**
  Doctrinal contribution: field norm — LOW signal
  Comparative law claim without systematic comparison: MEDIUM signal

**Field-specific signals:**
  Jurisdiction not specified for legal claims:
    Label: JURISDICTION_UNSPECIFIED
  Temporal scope of legal analysis not declared:
    Label: LEGAL_TEMPORAL_SCOPE_ABSENT

---

### 4.6 FIELD-ENG — Engineering and Applied Sciences

**Reproducibility & Open Science Kit (42) adjustments:**
  Code availability: HIGH concern
  Data availability: MEDIUM-HIGH concern
  Reporting standard: MEDIUM concern

**Uncertainty-Visibility Kit adjustments:**
  No error analysis: HIGH concern
  No confidence bounds on measurements: HIGH concern

**Overreach-Signal Kit adjustments:**
  TRANSLATIONAL_OVERREACH: HIGH sensitivity
    (laboratory result → deployment claim: HIGH)
  CONTRIBUTION_OVERREACH: MEDIUM sensitivity

**Field-specific signals:**
  Computational complexity not reported for algorithms:
    Label: COMPLEXITY_ABSENT
  Benchmark comparison absent:
    Label: BENCHMARK_ABSENT
  Dataset size and characteristics not reported:
    Label: DATASET_CHARACTERIZATION_ABSENT

---

### 4.7 FIELD-EDU — Education Sciences

**Reproducibility & Open Science Kit (42) adjustments:**
  Pre-registration: LOW-MEDIUM concern
  Data availability: MEDIUM concern (privacy constraints noted)

**Uncertainty-Visibility Kit adjustments:**
  No limitations section: MEDIUM concern
  Context-dependency not acknowledged: MEDIUM concern

**Overreach-Signal Kit adjustments:**
  GENERALIZATION_OVERREACH: HIGH sensitivity

  POLICY_OVERREACH: MEDIUM sensitivity

**Field-specific signals:**

## 5. USER-DEFINED FIELD TERMS

Users may register field-specific terms that affect signal behavior.
Terms are registered in this section and applied system-wide.

### 5.1 Field Term Registry Format

```
FIELD: [field code]
TERM: [term as it appears in manuscripts]
CONTEXT: [how it is used in this field]
SIGNAL_EFFECT: [SUPPRESS / AMPLIFY / RECLASSIFY]
SIGNAL_TARGET: [which kit signal is affected]
NOTE: [optional — why this term behaves differently here]
```

### 5.2 Example Entries

```
FIELD: FIELD-MED
TERM: significant
CONTEXT: p-value threshold reporting
SIGNAL_EFFECT: SUPPRESS
SIGNAL_TARGET: FREQUENCY_SIGNAL certainty escalation
NOTE: "significant" in statistical reporting is technical,
      not rhetorical certainty escalation.

FIELD: FIELD-HUM
TERM: demonstrates
CONTEXT: interpretive argument in humanities
SIGNAL_EFFECT: RECLASSIFY
SIGNAL_TARGET: CALIBRATION-DEEP over-claim detection §1.1
NOTE: "demonstrates" in humanities signals argumentative
      commitment, not empirical proof claim.
      Threshold raised from standard to MODERATE before flagging.

TERM: should
CONTEXT: legal analysis recommendation
SIGNAL_EFFECT: SUPPRESS

NOTE: "should" in doctrinal legal analysis is field-standard
      normative language, not policy overreach.
```

## 8. Q-VARIANT INTERACTION

Field calibration applies within Q-Gate thresholds.
Q-Gate takes precedence on tier-level sensitivity.
This kit takes precedence on field-level expectation.

Example: FIELD-MED + Q2
  Q2 sets conditional tolerance for most signals.
  FIELD-MED amplifies TRANSLATIONAL_OVERREACH to MAJOR
  regardless of Q2 conditional tolerance.
  The field expectation overrides tier tolerance
  for field-critical signals only.

Field calibration never suppresses a signal below Q1 minimum.
Field calibration never amplifies a signal above MAJOR severity.

---

## 9. ACTIVATION

Automatically attempts field detection for every report.
Field detection uses vocabulary, method, and citation signals.
User override accepted at any point.

If user provides journal name or ISSN:
→ Field mapped from journal → field detection confirmed
→ Noted in SECTION 0

---

## 10. PARTIAL INPUT BEHAVIOR

Abstract only (Type A):
→ Field detection attempted; confidence LOW
→ Field calibration applied at reduced sensitivity

Full manuscript:
→ Field detection high confidence
→ Full calibration applied

---

## 11. REVISION ROUND INTERACTION

In Revision Round Mode, field-critical signals
(FIELD-MED: TRANSLATIONAL_OVERREACH, CI_ABSENT;
 FIELD-SOC: RESPONSE_RATE_ABSENT;
 FIELD-ENG: BENCHMARK_ABSENT)
carry additional weight under Q1-level sensitivity.

Field-critical signals in revised manuscripts are read
as residual risk — these are the signals most frequently
raised in field-specific R1 reviewer comments.

## 13. COLLISION MANAGEMENT

Field calibration adjustments are applied before
kit signal production — they set thresholds,
not post-hoc modifiers. Collision management therefore
operates at the threshold level, not the output level.

If field calibration SUPPRESSES a signal that
another kit would produce:
→ Signal is not produced
→ No note to user (threshold was never crossed)

If field calibration AMPLIFIES a signal:
→ Signal is produced at amplified severity
→ FIELD SIGNAL label added to indicate field-specific weighting

If field calibration and Q-Gate conflict:
→ Q-Gate minimum always applies
→ Field calibration cannot suppress below Q-Gate minimum
→ Field calibration cannot amplify above MAJOR

---

## 14. WHAT THIS KIT DOES NOT DO

Does not:
  Replace Q-Gate tier calibration
  Evaluate whether a field's norms are appropriate
  Judge whether a manuscript meets field standards
  Recommend field-specific revisions
  Produce field-specific verdicts
  Override Calibration-Deep correction behavior

---

## 15. HARD LIMITS

This kit must never:
  Suppress a signal below Q1 minimum sensitivity
  Amplify a signal above MAJOR severity
  Override Calibration-Deep expression correction
  Produce field quality verdicts
  Label any manuscript as field-appropriate or field-inappropriate
  Generate independent sections

---

## 16. CANONICAL DEFINITION

JOURNAL-SENSITIVITY KIT v1.1 calibrates CLASR signal sensitivity
to field-specific publication norms across eight field categories —
adjusting signal thresholds for replication expectations,
uncertainty conventions, overreach sensitivity, and terminology —
adding field-specific signals where relevant — and providing
a user-fillable field term registry for domain-specific
terminology from ongoing manuscript scanning —
without modifying core signal taxonomy, Q-Gate thresholds,
or producing field quality verdicts.
