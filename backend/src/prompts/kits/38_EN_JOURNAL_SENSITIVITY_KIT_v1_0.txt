## 1. PURPOSE (ONE SENTENCE)

To calibrate CLASR-EN signal sensitivity to field-specific
publication norms, terminology conventions, and methodological
expectations — mapping where the same signal carries different
weight in different academic fields — without modifying
core signal taxonomy or Q-Gate thresholds.

---

## 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Q-Gate calibrates reading sensitivity to journal tier:
Q1 / Q2 / Q3 — how rigorous is the target journal.

This kit calibrates reading sensitivity to journal field:
what does rigorous mean in this specific domain.

Q-Gate governs tier sensitivity.
This kit governs field sensitivity.
Both active simultaneously. Not merged.

---

## 3. FIELD TAXONOMY

Eight broad field categories:

```
FIELD-NAT   → Natural Sciences
              (biology, chemistry, physics, earth sciences)

FIELD-MED   → Medical and Health Sciences
              (clinical, epidemiology, public health, pharmacology)

FIELD-SOC   → Social Sciences
              (sociology, political science, economics, psychology)

FIELD-HUM   → Humanities
              (history, literature, philosophy, cultural studies)

FIELD-LAW   → Legal and Regulatory
              (law, compliance, policy analysis)

FIELD-ENG   → Engineering and Applied Sciences
              (computer science, materials, environmental engineering)

FIELD-EDU   → Education Sciences
              (pedagogy, curriculum, learning sciences)

FIELD-INT   → Interdisciplinary
              (mixed field signals; no single field dominant)
```

Field is detected from manuscript vocabulary, citation patterns,
method names, and statistical approaches.

If field cannot be determined:
→ FIELD-INT applied as default
→ Noted in SECTION 0

## 4. FIELD-SPECIFIC SIGNAL CALIBRATION

### 4.1 FIELD-NAT — Natural Sciences

Replication Signal Kit: pre-registration LOW concern (not field norm);
  code availability HIGH concern for computational studies.
Uncertainty-Visibility: no dedicated limitations section LOW concern
  (limitations typically embedded in discussion).
Contribution framing: incremental contribution is field norm — LOW signal.

### 4.2 FIELD-MED — Medical and Health Sciences

Replication Signal Kit: pre-registration HIGH concern (clinical norm);
  ethics declaration MANDATORY — absence is HIGH signal;
  reporting standard (CONSORT/STROBE) HIGH concern.
Overreach-Signal Kit: TRANSLATIONAL_OVERREACH maximum sensitivity
  (in vitro → clinical claim: always MAJOR).
POLICY_OVERREACH: HIGH sensitivity.
Field-specific signals:
  RISK_FRAMING_SIGNAL — relative risk only, no absolute figures.
  NNT_ABSENT_SIGNAL — number needed to treat absent from clinical claims.
  CI_ABSENT_SIGNAL — p-value only, no confidence intervals.

### 4.3 FIELD-SOC — Social Sciences

Replication Signal Kit: pre-registration MEDIUM concern (growing norm).
Overreach-Signal Kit: GENERALIZATION_OVERREACH HIGH sensitivity
  (single-country study → universal claim: MAJOR).
Field-specific signals:
  RESPONSE_RATE_ABSENT — survey response rate not reported.
  SAMPLING_FRAME_ABSENT — sampling frame not described.

### 4.4 FIELD-HUM — Humanities

Replication Signal Kit: all dimensions NOT APPLICABLE (field norm).
Uncertainty-Visibility: no formal limitations section LOW concern.
Frequency Signal: "demonstrates" in argument context — lower threshold.
Normative language: MEDIUM concern (humanities has normative tradition).
Field-specific signals:
  PRIMARY_SOURCE_ABSENT — primary source citation absent for historical claims.
  ARCHIVAL_BASIS_ABSENT — archival basis not described for historical argument.

### 4.5 FIELD-LAW — Legal and Regulatory

Replication Signal Kit: all dimensions NOT APPLICABLE (field norm).
POLICY_OVERREACH: LOW sensitivity (policy prescription is field norm).
"should" / "must" in legal analysis: LOW concern (field norm).
Field-specific signals:
  JURISDICTION_UNSPECIFIED — jurisdiction not specified for legal claims.
  LEGAL_TEMPORAL_SCOPE_ABSENT — temporal scope of legal analysis not declared.

### 4.6 FIELD-ENG — Engineering and Applied Sciences

Replication Signal Kit: code availability HIGH concern.
Uncertainty-Visibility: no error analysis HIGH concern.
TRANSLATIONAL_OVERREACH: HIGH sensitivity (lab result → deployment claim).
Field-specific signals:
  COMPLEXITY_ABSENT — computational complexity not reported for algorithms.
  BENCHMARK_ABSENT — benchmark comparison absent.
  DATASET_CHARACTERIZATION_ABSENT — dataset size and characteristics not reported.

### 4.7 FIELD-EDU — Education Sciences

GENERALIZATION_OVERREACH: HIGH sensitivity
  (single-school findings → general claim: HIGH).
Field-specific signals:
  INTERVENTION_FIDELITY_ABSENT — intervention fidelity not reported.
  INSTRUCTOR_EFFECT_ABSENT — teacher/instructor effect not addressed.

### 4.8 FIELD-INT — Interdisciplinary

Default profile: standard CLASR-EN thresholds.
No field-specific suppression or amplification.
Interdisciplinary Tension Kit: MAXIMUM sensitivity.

## 7. Q-VARIANT INTERACTION

Field calibration applies within Q-Gate thresholds.
Q-Gate takes precedence on tier-level sensitivity.
This kit takes precedence on field-level expectation.

Field calibration never suppresses a signal below Q1 minimum.
Field calibration never amplifies a signal above MAJOR severity.

---

## 8. ACTIVATION

Automatically attempts field detection for every report.
User override accepted at any point.

---

## 9. HARD LIMITS

This kit must never:
  Suppress a signal below Q1 minimum sensitivity
  Amplify a signal above MAJOR severity
  Override Calibration-Deep expression correction
  Produce field quality verdicts
  Label any manuscript as field-appropriate or field-inappropriate
  Generate independent sections

---

## 10. CANONICAL DEFINITION

JOURNAL-SENSITIVITY KIT v1.0 calibrates CLASR-EN signal sensitivity
to field-specific publication norms across eight field categories —
adjusting signal thresholds for replication expectations,
uncertainty conventions, overreach sensitivity, and terminology —
adding field-specific signals where relevant —
without modifying core signal taxonomy, Q-Gate thresholds,
or producing field quality verdicts.
