## 1. PURPOSE (ONE SENTENCE)

To surface whether the claims made in the conclusion
are traceable to the results and discussion —
mapping new claim introduction, contribution inflation,
and result-conclusion disproportionality as structural
integrity signals, without producing evaluation.

---

## 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Argument Chain Kit tracks the central claim longitudinally
across four transitions. Discussion Scope Drift Kit reads
the Results → Discussion directional distance.

Neither reads the conclusion zone specifically.

The conclusion is a structurally distinct zone:
it is the manuscript's final public claim — the statement
that remains in the reader's memory, that gets cited,
that shapes how the manuscript is positioned in a field.

When the conclusion introduces claims not traceable
to the results, or inflates contributions beyond
what the analysis supports, this is a structural
integrity signal regardless of what the rest
of the manuscript does correctly.

```
Results zone signals extracted
         ↓
Discussion zone signals extracted
         ↓
Conclusion zone signals extracted
         ↓
Traceability map assembled:
  each conclusion claim → results source
         ↓
New introductions and inflations identified
         ↓
Integrity profile reported — no verdict
```

---

## 3. CONCLUSION ZONE DEFINITION

The conclusion zone includes:
- Explicitly labeled Conclusion section
- Concluding Remarks or Summary section
- Final paragraphs of Discussion when no separate Conclusion exists
- Abstract conclusion statement (read separately — see § 7)

The conclusion zone does not include:
- Implications sections read as Discussion continuations
- Future work sections (read separately by existing kits)
- Acknowledgments or declarations

---

## 4. SIGNALS READ

### 4.1 New Claim Introduction

A claim appears in the conclusion that was not
present in results or discussion.

Signal indicators:
- Concepts named in conclusion not appearing in methods or results
- Causal claims in conclusion absent from results
- Population-level claims in conclusion
  where results were sample-bounded
- "This study shows that X affects Y"
  where no results section addressed X-Y relationship

Label: CONCLUSION_NEW_CLAIM
Magnitude: [MINOR / MODERATE / MAJOR]

MINOR: single hedged claim extension
MODERATE: clear new claim without results anchor
MAJOR: conclusion reframes the manuscript's
       central finding in a new direction

---

### 4.2 Contribution Inflation in Conclusion

The conclusion's contribution claim exceeds
what the study's design, scope, or data can support —
specifically in relation to what was delivered
in the results and discussion.

Signal indicators:
- "This study makes a major contribution to..."
  where results were exploratory or descriptive
- "These findings transform our understanding of..."
  where findings were incremental
- Theoretical contribution claimed from empirical study
  without theoretical framework in the body
- "Universal implications" from bounded sample

Label: CONCLUSION_CONTRIBUTION_INFLATION
Intensity: [LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH]

---

### 4.3 Result-Conclusion Disproportionality

The conclusion is significantly denser in claim weight
than the results it summarizes.

Signal indicators:
- Conclusion introduces 3+ new implications
  for every 1 result reported
- Conclusion discusses applications in domains
  not addressed in the study design
- Results presented as tentative;
  conclusion presents them as established

Label: RESULT_CONCLUSION_DISPROPORTION
Direction: [CONCLUSION_EXCEEDS / CONCLUSION_UNDERSELLS]

CONCLUSION_EXCEEDS: conclusion inflates beyond results
CONCLUSION_UNDERSELLS: conclusion retreats from
  what results actually support
  (self-suppression pattern — less common but notable)

---

### 4.4 Limitation Reversal

The manuscript acknowledges limitations in SECTION 8
but the conclusion proceeds as if those limitations
do not apply to its claims.

Signal indicators:
- SECTION 8: "limited to a single region"
  Conclusion: "these findings apply broadly to..."
- SECTION 8: "cross-sectional design limits causality"
  Conclusion: "this demonstrates that X causes Y"
- Limitation acknowledged then functionally ignored
  in the conclusion's claim framing

Label: LIMITATION_REVERSAL

---

### 4.5 Abstract-Conclusion Consistency

The conclusion's central claim compared
to the abstract's conclusion statement.

States:
```
CONSISTENT    → abstract and conclusion carry the same claim
ESCALATED     → conclusion claim stronger than abstract
DEFLATED      → conclusion claim weaker than abstract
SUBSTITUTED   → conclusion introduces a different central claim
               than the abstract declared
```

## 6. Q-VARIANT CALIBRATION

Q1:
  All five signal types scanned at full sensitivity.
  CONCLUSION_NEW_CLAIM at MODERATE or MAJOR explicitly surfaced.
  LIMITATION_REVERSAL noted as potential reviewer concern.
  SUBSTITUTED abstract-conclusion noted as desk-reject signal.

  CONCLUSION_NEW_CLAIM at MAJOR noted.

## 7. PARTIAL INPUT BEHAVIOR

Abstract only (Type A):
→ Conclusion not available — kit runs silently

Introduction only (Type B):
→ Kit runs silently

Methods only (Type C):
→ Kit runs silently

Results / Discussion only (Type D):
→ If conclusion zone present: full scan
→ Abstract-conclusion consistency: reduced coverage

Mixed (Type E):
→ Kit runs on available conclusion zone
→ Coverage noted

---

## 8. REVISION ROUND INTERACTION

In Revision Round Mode, conclusion integrity signals
carry additional weight under Q1-level sensitivity.

CONCLUSION_NEW_CLAIM and LIMITATION_REVERSAL
in revised manuscripts are read as residual risk.

SUBSTITUTED abstract-conclusion in a revised manuscript
carries particular weight: it may indicate that the revision
changed the manuscript's central claim without updating
the abstract accordingly.

No separate flag produced.
Signal reported as normal; sensitivity applies implicitly.

## 9. INTEGRATION — LENS-BRIDGE CONNECTION

### Primary: SECTION 4 — Argumentation

→ positioned after argument chain signals

### Secondary: SECTION 8 — Limits & Uncertainties

the gap between acknowledged limitations
and conclusion claims constitutes an unmapped
knowledge boundary
→ flagged as limitation-conclusion gap within SECTION 8

### Secondary: SECTION 0 — Macro Frame
Where SUBSTITUTED abstract-conclusion or MAJOR
new claim introduction affects the manuscript's
overall behavioral profile
→ noted as framing-conclusion gap within SECTION 0

## 10. COLLISION MANAGEMENT

If CONCLUSION_NEW_CLAIM overlaps with
Argument Chain Kit CLAIM_SUBSTITUTION:
→ Argument Chain Kit reports intra-body substitution;
  Conclusion Integrity Kit reports conclusion-zone introduction
→ Both active in SECTION 4 as separate sub-signals

If CONCLUSION_CONTRIBUTION_INFLATION overlaps with
Contribution Framing Kit CONTRIBUTION_SCOPE_INFLATION:
→ Contribution Kit reads framing zone inflation;
  Conclusion Integrity Kit reads conclusion-zone inflation
→ Both active; reported in their respective SECTIONs

If LIMITATION_REVERSAL overlaps with
Argument Symmetry Kit ASYMMETRIC CLOSING zone:
→ Symmetry Kit reports zone-level ratio;
  Conclusion Integrity Kit reports specific reversal instance
→ Both active; reported as separate sub-signals

If abstract-conclusion signal overlaps with
Abstract-Body Coherence Kit CLAIM_ESCALATION:
→ Coherence Kit takes precedence on abstract-body distance;
  Conclusion Integrity Kit reports conclusion-zone profile
→ Not merged

---

## 11. WHAT THIS KIT DOES NOT DO

Does not:
  Evaluate whether the conclusion's claims are correct
  Judge whether contribution claims are significant
  Recommend how to revise the conclusion
  Label the conclusion as strong or weak
  Generate independent flags outside SECTION 4, SECTION 8, SECTION 0

---

## 12. HARD LIMITS

This kit must never:
  Evaluate the scholarly significance of conclusions
  Recommend specific conclusion revisions
  Label conclusions as over-reaching or under-reaching
  Produce a conclusion quality score
  Act as a standalone conclusion review tool

---

## 13. CANONICAL DEFINITION

CONCLUSION INTEGRITY KIT v1.0 surfaces whether the claims
made in the conclusion are traceable to results and discussion —
mapping new claim introduction, contribution inflation,
result-conclusion disproportionality, limitation reversal,
and abstract-conclusion consistency — as structural integrity
signals, without producing judgment, evaluation, or recommendation.
