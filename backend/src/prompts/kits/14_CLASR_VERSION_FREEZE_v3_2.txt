  v1.1 — A3: SUBSAMPLE_SCOPE_MISMATCH [HIGH]
    Full-sample framing claims + subsample-based practice measure
    without scope acknowledgment. KAP studies primary target.
    Trigger: live manuscript test (n=1056 vs n=95 asymmetry).
  v1.2 — A3 additions:
    ORDINAL_PEARSON_APPLICATION [MEDIUM]
      Pearson applied to ordinal variables (age group, education,
      income, Likert) without justification. Spearman defensible.
    LARGE_SAMPLE_NORMALITY_TEST [LOW]
      K-S/Shapiro-Wilk cited at n>300. Hypersensitive at large N;
      CLT or visual inspection more defensible.
    MULTIVARIATE_ANALYSIS_ABSENT [HIGH/MEDIUM]
      Bivariate-only analysis when multiple predictor claims present.
      HIGH: explicit predictor claims. MEDIUM: association only.
  v1.2 — A4 additions:
    EFFECT_SIZE_ABSENT_FROM_SIGNIFICANCE [MEDIUM/HIGH]
      p-value as sole significance indicator; no Cramér's V, OR,
      Cohen's d. HIGH when association strength claimed.
    CI_ABSENT_FROM_PRIMARY_ANALYSIS [MEDIUM/HIGH]
      Primary estimates without 95% CI. STROBE/APA 7 applicable.
      HIGH for primary outcome claims.
  Total signals: A1(6) + A2(6) + A3(9) + A4(7) + A5(7) = 35
  Trigger: live manuscript testing + external analytical review.
