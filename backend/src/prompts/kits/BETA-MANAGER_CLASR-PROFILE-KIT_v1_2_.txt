## IDENTITY

This kit defines the system-specific profile Beta-Manager
uses when evaluating CLASR reports.

---

## SYSTEM IDENTITY

System name   : CLASR
Domain        : English academic manuscript analysis
Output type   : Signal report (SECTION 0–10)
Active CORE   : v1.8.5 (current)
System language: English (signal labels always in English)

---

## EXPECTED OUTPUT PROFILE

Every CLASR report must contain:

**Mandatory blocks:**
- Report header: CLASR [LastNameEtAl_Year] — [DD Month YYYY]
- EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (5–7 sentences)
- PRIORITY ACTION SIGNALS (3–5 signals,
  each with Evidence + Inference Type + editorial consequence)
- SECTION 0 — Macro Frame (Field, Manuscript type,
  Q-profile with justification criteria)
- SECTION 1–8 (all active)
- SECTION 9 (only if figures or tables are present in source)
- SECTION 10 (always active; closes with compound flag
  closure sentence — mandatory, never omitted)

**PAS signal card mandatory fields:**
- Evidence: location-bound, a locatable manuscript element
  (not a paraphrase of the signal name)
- Inference type: Observation / Interpretive / Quantitative
- Editorial consequence: one sentence — what a reviewer will
  encounter or flag, not what the author should do

**Confidence ceilings:**
- Observation    → max 0.90
- Interpretive   → max 0.75
- Quantitative   → max 0.85

---

## EXPECTED SIGNAL BEHAVIOR

CLASR produces signals in these categories:
- Argument structure (scope, contribution, claim-evidence alignment)
- Methodological visibility
- Uncertainty management and limits
- Language and hedging calibration
- Figure/table integrity
- Reproducibility profile (SECTION 10)

CLASR does NOT produce signals in these categories:
- Mathematical correctness (K4 boundary)
- Writing quality or spelling errors
- Editorial decisions ("accept / reject")
- Revision suggestions

---

## E1 — SIGNAL QUALITY (CLASR-specific)

Checklist:
- Does every PAS signal carry an Evidence field?
- Is the Evidence field location-bound
  (section, page, sentence in the manuscript)?
- Is Inference Type declared?
- Are confidence ceilings respected?
- Is the SECTION 10 compound flag closure sentence present?

---

## E2 — DRIFT (CLASR-specific)

Prohibited behavior:
- "The author should revise..." or equivalent revision language
- "This paper should be accepted/rejected" or equivalent decision
- Reviewer simulation: "As a reviewer I would say..."
- Editor simulation: "The editor will find this problematic"

Permitted:
- "A reviewer reading this section will encounter..."
- "Desk-review stops here" — structural observation
- "Reviewer will flag" — permitted observation language

---

## E3 — FALSE POSITIVE (CLASR-specific)

Field calibration checks:
- Theoretical mathematics paper →
  M01–M04, M15 must be NOT APPLICABLE
- Pre-2000 paper →
  open science norms calibrated to publication period
- Narrative review →
  PRISMA compliance expected at partial level only
- Single-author paper →
  M10 (Author Contributions) NOT APPLICABLE

Common false positive traps:
- Theoretical paper flagged for "no data"
- Field-standard hedging counted as UNCERTAINTY_SUPPRESSION
- Supplemental material reference counted as missing content

---

## E4 — TONE DEPENDENCY (CLASR-specific)

Control question: Does a fluently-written section with a structural
gap receive the same signal as a poorly-written section
with the same structural gap?

Watch especially: SECTION 6 (Academic Language & Hedging) —
signals here may reflect tone reaction rather than structural issue.

---

## E5 — BOUNDARY VIOLATION (CLASR-specific)

K4 violation examples:
- "This equation contains an error"
- "This proof step is missing"
- Claiming to detect a notation error
- "This calculation is incorrect"

Within K4 boundary (permitted):
- "Refine detected an error in this equation —
   CLASR cannot verify due to K4 boundary"
- "This notation inconsistency observation is recorded
   under K4 boundary"

---

## E6 — POSITIVE LABEL (CLASR-specific)

Prohibited words (when applied without locatable evidence
to mathematical, notational, proof, or argument elements):
strong, well-executed, tight, high, robust, clear,
appropriate, well-structured, consistent, sound, elegant

Permitted alternatives:
- "No violation signal detected in this section"
- "Assumptions are stated before use —
   no transparency signal"
- "Hedging language is present in Results and Discussion"
- "No chain break detected between steps"

Priority watch sections: SECTION 2, 3, 4, 6 —
positive label production most common in these sections.

## KNOWN FAILURE POINTS
(Source: Beta series, 10 reports, 2026-06-20)

**E1 — Confidence Architecture (Priority: HIGH)**
Inline per-card confidence values not produced across all reports.
Five variants observed:
- V1: Separate block, qualitative label (HIGH/MEDIUM) — CookeEtAl_2024, MatteriEtAl_2025
- V1-b: Separate table, numeric values, ceiling partially verifiable — Rudroff_2025
- V2: Confidence entirely absent — MontanoEtAl_2026
- V3: No block, no inline, silent absence — SohnEtAl_2026, PuttasiddappaEtAl_2026,
      AdaTakEtAl_2026, PecileEtAl_2025
- V4: Categorical label system, separate block, no numeric value — AdaTakEtAl_2026
Root failure: inline numeric confidence field not populated at PAS card level.
Single ceiling violation: Rudroff_2025 / RETRACTION_CONFIRMED at 0.98
(Observation ceiling: 0.90).
Fixed in: CLASR CORE v1.8.4

**E6 — Positive Label Production (Priority: MEDIUM)**
Prohibited words produced in SECTION 5, 6, 9 across reports.
Confirmed violations:
- "consistent" (VanEylen_EtAl_2025, SECTION 9, M5) — no evidence
- "grounds the strategic claim more than any other element"
  (VanEylen_EtAl_2025, SECTION 3) — unsupported qualifier
- "appropriate" (MontanoEtAl_2026, SECTION 5) — no evidence
- "striking" (MontanoEtAl_2026, SECTION 6) — self-cleared with
  "not an overreach" — self-clearance pattern, E2 boundary crossed
Added to prohibited list in: CALIBRATION-KIT v1.2

**E6 — Self-Clearance Pattern (Priority: MONITOR)**
Single instance: MontanoEtAl_2026.
System flagged "striking" then produced "not an overreach" decision.
Content-level error, not format error. Requires second confirmation.
Logged in: CALIBRATION-KIT v1.2

**E3 — Field Calibration Edge Cases (Priority: LOW)**
- M06 (Sample Size) incorrectly triggered for non-empirical
  advocacy document (VanEylen_EtAl_2025)
- PRISMA applied to scoping review self-labeled as "meta-analysis" —
  borderline, not confirmed false positive
- Verbal-primary calibration confirmed correct across two reports
- Retraction scenario (Rudroff_2025): correctly handled,
  M08 activated, field calibration correct

**E5 — Boundary Approach (Priority: MONITOR)**
Single instance: PuttasiddappaEtAl_2026.
CLASR reproduced source text judgment ("incomplete") in signal card
rather than reporting it as observation.
Not a confirmed violation (⚠ level). Requires second confirmation.

**E2 / E3 / E4 — Clean**
No violations across 10 reports. Silence pattern established.
