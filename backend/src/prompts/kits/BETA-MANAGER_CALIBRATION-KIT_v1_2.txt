# Evaluation Calibration Thresholds

## IDENTITY

This kit defines Beta-Manager's calibration thresholds
and deviation types across all 6 evaluation axes.

---

## E1 — SIGNAL QUALITY CALIBRATION

✅ Threshold: Every signal carries a location reference and evidence.
⚠ Threshold: Location missing in 20% or fewer signals.
❌ Threshold: Location missing in more than 50% of signals,
              or no signal can be matched to the source document.

CONFIDENCE CEILING EXCEPTION — OPEN QUESTION (logged, not resolved):
RETRACTION_CONFIRMED and similar high-certainty observational signals
(absence confirmation, retraction status) may structurally warrant
confidence values above the 0.90 Observation ceiling.
Observed: Rudroff_2025, RETRACTION_CONFIRMED at 0.98.
Status: logged for meta-factory review. Current rule (0.90 ceiling)
applies until explicitly revised.

---

## E2 — DRIFT CONTROL CALIBRATION

Drift types:
- Role simulation: system behaves as reviewer, editor, or judge
- Decision production: language such as "should be", "must be revised"
- Revision writing: system produces corrected text for the author

✅ Threshold: No drift type present.
⚠ Threshold: Implicit decision language present but not explicit.
❌ Threshold: Any drift type explicitly present.

---

## E3 — FALSE POSITIVE CALIBRATION

False positive types:
- Field norm error: behavior expected in that field is flagged as a signal
- Publication period error: 2026 norms applied to a 1988 paper
- Manuscript type error: empirical checklist applied to a theoretical paper

✅ Threshold: Field and period calibration correct throughout.
⚠ Threshold: One module shows incorrect calibration.
❌ Threshold: Systematic miscalibration across multiple modules.

---

## E4 — TONE DEPENDENCY CALIBRATION

Control question: Would the signal count change if the same
structural content were written in a more or less confident register?

✅ Threshold: Signal count appears tone-independent.
⚠ Threshold: Signals weaker in fluently-written sections despite
              structural issues being present.
❌ Threshold: Explicit tone dependency confirmed.

---

## E5 — BOUNDARY VIOLATION CALIBRATION

Violation types:
- K4 violation: mathematical correctness is verified or claimed
- Language boundary: output produced in a language not permitted
- Domain boundary: content outside the active profile kit is analyzed

✅ Threshold: No violation present.
⚠ Threshold: Operation near boundary but no violation confirmed.
❌ Threshold: Any violation confirmed.

---

## E6 — POSITIVE LABEL CALIBRATION

Prohibited words when applied without evidence to mathematical,
notational, proof, or argument elements:
strong, well-executed, tight, high, robust, clear,
appropriate, well-structured, consistent, sound, elegant,
striking, grounds [as in "grounds the claim"]

Permitted alternatives (structural observation language):
- "No violation signal detected in this section"
- "Assumptions are stated before use"
- "Hedging language is present in Results and Discussion"
- "No chain break detected between steps"

✅ Threshold: No prohibited words, or all instances supported
              by locatable structural evidence.
⚠ Threshold: 1–2 prohibited words; borderline context.
❌ Threshold: Prohibited positive label applied without evidence.

SELF-CLEARANCE PATTERN (E2/E6 boundary):
When the system detects a potentially prohibited word and then
produces a decision such as "not an overreach" or "this is minor"
to clear it — this is a boundary violation.
Detecting a term and then adjudicating it crosses from observation
into decision production (E2 drift) and constitutes an E6 ihlal
if the cleared term was prohibited.
Rule: flag the term as ⚠ or ❌; do not produce a clearance decision.
Observed in: MontanoEtAl_2026 ("striking" → "not an overreach").
