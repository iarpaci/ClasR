## 0. IDENTITY

Type: EXTENSION KIT
Attaches to: CLASR reading layer (CORE unchanged)
No content generation · No judgment · No recommendation

This kit identifies, maps, and signals epistemic frame behavior
in English academic manuscripts.

It does not evaluate which epistemological framework is correct.
It surfaces how frameworks operate, where they are visible,
and where they generate internal tensions with method or contribution.

**Scope note:**
This kit activates for all manuscript types.
In QUANTITATIVE-PRIMARY manuscripts, epistemic frame signals
are most visible in framing zones (introduction, discussion).
In VERBAL-PRIMARY manuscripts, epistemic frame signals
are active across all zones.
In HYBRID manuscripts, frame-method alignment is the
primary signal zone.

---

## 1. EPISTEMIC FRAME TAXONOMY

Six frame types are recognized. Detection is operational —
what the manuscript actually does, not what the author declares.

| Frame Type | Core Assumption | Common Markers |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| Post-positivist | Reality exists; approximated through controlled inquiry | findings, evidence, validity, generalizability, reliability |
| Interpretivist | Meaning is socially constructed; understanding is contextual | meaning, understanding, lived experience, interpretation, context |
| Critical | Power structures shape knowledge; research should expose and challenge them | power, emancipation, oppression, ideology, structural, critique |
| Post-structuralist | Categories and meanings are unstable; deconstruction is primary | discourse, subject position, genealogy, deconstruction, difference |
| Pragmatist | Knowledge judged by practical utility and consequence | utility, action, practice, consequence, what works |
| Mixed / Unspecified | Multiple frames or no declared frame | Marker vocabulary spans multiple frame types without synthesis |

**Detection method:**
Frame is detected through five channels:
1. Vocabulary pattern — which frame-specific terms appear and with what density
2. Ontological claims — what the manuscript asserts about the nature of reality or knowledge
3. Methodological logic — what counts as adequate grounding for a claim
4. Subject construction — how actors, agents, and social entities are conceptualized
5. Contribution framing — how novelty and significance are defined

Detected frame = operational frame.
If declared frame and operational frame diverge, both are noted.
The operational frame is the primary signal.

## 2. FRAME VISIBILITY

How explicitly the epistemic frame is positioned:

```
EXPLICIT   → manuscript declares its epistemological position
             in dedicated framing language
IMPLICIT   → frame detectable through vocabulary and logic
             but not declared by the author
INVISIBLE  → manuscript operates as if epistemologically neutral;
             no positioning statement present or implied
```

Visibility state is reported without judgment.
INVISIBLE is a signal, not a verdict.
Some fields have low frame-declaration norms —
visibility is read against disciplinary expectation,
not against a universal standard.

---

## 3. FRAME-METHOD ALIGNMENT

Reads whether the methodological approach is consistent
with the detected epistemic frame.

| State | Definition | Signal level |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| ALIGNED | Detected frame and methodological approach are consistent | No signal — expected behavior |
| PARTIAL_TENSION | Frame and method partially misalign | MEDIUM |
| MISALIGNED | Clear inconsistency between operational frame and practice | HIGH |

**Examples of misalignment:**
- Manuscript declares interpretivist stance but applies
  positivist validity criteria (reliability, generalizability)
- Post-structuralist vocabulary in framing but causal claims in findings
- Critical framework claimed but power analysis absent from discussion
- Pragmatist framing but no engagement with practical consequences
- Interpretivist framing but sample size justified with power analysis logic

**Misalignment location:**
When MISALIGNED, the specific zone where the inconsistency
appears is noted (framing zone / methods zone / discussion zone).

---

## 4. FRAME-CONTRIBUTION ALIGNMENT

Reads whether the manuscript's contribution claim
is consistent with what its epistemic frame can deliver.

| State | Example | Signal level |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| ALIGNED | Interpretivist manuscript claims new contextual understanding | No signal |
| PARTIAL_TENSION | Critical manuscript claims empirical contribution without frame bridge | MEDIUM |
| MISALIGNED | Post-structuralist manuscript claims definitive findings | HIGH |

Additional misalignment patterns:
- Post-positivist manuscript claims theoretical contribution
  without engaging theoretical literature
- Interpretivist manuscript claims generalizability
  without acknowledging frame-based scope limits
- Critical manuscript claims policy application
  without addressing power-in-implementation

---

## 5. FRAME UNIVERSALIZATION

Detected when one epistemic frame is presented as
self-evidently correct without acknowledgment of alternatives.

**Signal indicators:**
- "Research must be..." (followed by frame-specific criterion)
- "True understanding requires..." (where 'true' implies frame-specific standard)
- "Rigorous analysis demands..." (where 'rigorous' is defined by one tradition)
- Alternative epistemologies unnamed and unengaged
- Methodological choices presented as neutral or obvious
  when they reflect specific epistemic commitments

**Label:** FRAME_UNIVERSALIZATION
**Intensity:** [LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH]

LOW: single instance; minor framing language
MEDIUM: recurring pattern; alternatives consistently absent
HIGH: frame treated as the only valid approach throughout;
  no acknowledgment that other frameworks exist

Cross-reference with SECTION 4 (Argumentation):
Frame universalization often functions as an argumentative
closure mechanism — eliminating alternatives before
the argument has established why they are excluded.
When this occurs, ORIENTATION LENS KIT also fires.
Both reported; not merged.

---

## 6. STRUCTURAL NORMATIVE DEPTH

Three patterns of epistemically embedded normativity
that operate below the level of explicit normative language:

### 6.1 Alternative Erasure
Competing frameworks are absent or dismissed
without substantive engagement.

**Detection method:**
Trace what is not present where alternative engagement
is structurally expected — literature review zone,
theoretical framework zone, methodology justification zone.

**Signal:** ALTERNATIVE_ERASURE
Distinct from Silence Lens FRAMEWORK_MONOPOLY:
Silence Lens reads argumentative silence.
This pattern reads epistemic silence — the systematic
exclusion of epistemological alternatives, not just
argumentative alternatives.
Both may fire; reported as separate sub-signals.

---

### 6.2 Inevitability Architecture
Manuscript builds toward a conclusion framed as
the only logical outcome.

**Detection method:**
Track the logic chain from framing to conclusion.
Note where branching closes — where the argument
stops acknowledging that other conclusions were possible.

**Signal:** INEVITABILITY_ARCHITECTURE
Distinct from ORIENTATION LENS "only option" framing:
Orientation Lens reads scope-setting behaviors at the
aim and method level.
This pattern reads the full argument architecture —
how the conclusion is constructed as inevitable
across the manuscript's entire trajectory.
Both may fire; reported in their respective sections.

---

### 6.3 Epistemic Privilege
One way of knowing is treated as universally valid —
methodological choices presented as neutral or obvious
when they reflect specific epistemic commitments.

**Detection method:**
Detect where methodological choices are presented
without epistemological justification — where the
choice is treated as self-evident rather than
as one defensible option among others.

**Signal:** EPISTEMIC_PRIVILEGE
Relationship to FRAME_UNIVERSALIZATION:
Frame Universalization: frame presented as the only valid frame.
Epistemic Privilege: specific methodological practices treated
as epistemically neutral when they are not.
Both may co-occur; reported as separate signals.

## 9. Q-VARIANT CALIBRATION

**Q1:**
All signals active at full sensitivity.
INVISIBLE frame visibility explicitly surfaced — Q1 reviewers
weight epistemological positioning heavily in theoretical work.
MISALIGNED frame-method noted as potential desk-reject signal.
FRAME_UNIVERSALIZATION at MEDIUM and HIGH explicitly surfaced.
All three structural normative depth patterns active.

**Q2:**
All signals active at standard sensitivity.
PARTIAL_TENSION states noted without escalation.
INVISIBLE frame visibility noted where disciplinary norm expects declaration.
FRAME_UNIVERSALIZATION at HIGH explicitly surfaced.

**Q3:**
MISALIGNED states reported.
FRAME_UNIVERSALIZATION at HIGH reported.
PARTIAL_TENSION states suppressed silently.
INVISIBLE frame visibility suppressed unless manuscript
explicitly claims an epistemological position elsewhere.
Structural normative depth: INEVITABILITY_ARCHITECTURE
and ALTERNATIVE_ERASURE at HIGH reported; EPISTEMIC_PRIVILEGE
at standard sensitivity.

## 10. PARTIAL INPUT BEHAVIOR

**Abstract only (Type A):**
→ Frame vocabulary detectable from abstract
→ Frame visibility assessable
→ Alignment states: LOW confidence
→ Label: [Epistemic frame — abstract only, alignment confidence LOW]

**Introduction only (Type B):**
→ Frame detection: full or near-full
→ Frame-method alignment: partial (methods absent)
→ Structural normative depth: partially readable

**Methods only (Type C):**
→ Frame-method alignment: fully readable
→ Frame detection: partial
→ Epistemic privilege: fully readable

**Results / Discussion only (Type D):**
→ Frame-contribution alignment: fully readable
→ Inevitability architecture: readable in discussion
→ Frame detection: partial

**Mixed (Type E):**
→ Kit runs on available zones
→ Coverage declared per signal type

## 11. COLLISION MANAGEMENT

### With Interdisciplinary Tension Kit (27):

Interdisciplinary Tension Kit reads tradition-level conflict
between disciplines (e.g., positivist method + interpretivist theory
from different fields).

This kit reads epistemic frame coherence within the manuscript's
own declared position.

Both may fire simultaneously:
→ Interdisciplinary Tension Kit: TENSION_SUPPRESSED in SECTION 2
→ This kit: MISALIGNED in SECTION 3
→ Both active; reported as separate sub-signals; not merged.

---

### With Orientation Lens Kit (16):

Orientation Lens reads "only option" framing at aim/scope level.
This kit reads frame universalization across the full manuscript.

Both may fire:
→ Orientation Lens: ONLY-OPTION FRAMING in SECTION 1
→ This kit: FRAME_UNIVERSALIZATION in SECTION 2
→ Both active; reported in their respective sections; not merged.

---

### With Silence Lens Kit (15):

Silence Lens reads FRAMEWORK_MONOPOLY as argumentative silence.
This kit reads ALTERNATIVE_ERASURE as epistemic silence.

Both may fire on the same zone:
→ Silence Lens: FRAMEWORK_MONOPOLY in SECTION 7
→ This kit: ALTERNATIVE_ERASURE in SECTION 2
→ More specific signal takes precedence in its section.
→ Not merged; collision not surfaced to user.

---

### With Hedging Calibration Kit (37):

Hedging Calibration Kit reads inconsistent hedging across claim types.
This kit reads frame-contribution misalignment.

Where both fire (e.g., post-structuralist framing +
unhedged causal conclusion):
→ Hedging Calibration Kit: CALIBRATION_FAILURE in SECTION 6
→ This kit: FRAME-CONTRIBUTION MISALIGNED in SECTION 4
→ Both active; not merged.

---

### With Verbal Lens Kit (10) Independent Mode:

In VERBAL-PRIMARY manuscripts, Verbal Lens Independent Mode
reads epistemic overreach at the language level.
This kit reads epistemic frame behavior at the structural level.

Both active simultaneously:
→ Verbal Lens: EPISTEMIC_OVERREACH in SECTION 6
→ This kit: FRAME_UNIVERSALIZATION or MISALIGNED in SECTION 2/3
→ Complementary; different levels of analysis; not merged.

---

## 12. REVISION ROUND INTERACTION

In Revision Round Mode, epistemic frame signals carry
additional weight under Q1-level sensitivity.

MISALIGNED frame-method in revised manuscripts is read
as residual risk — epistemological coherence is frequently
raised in R1 reviewer comments for theoretical and
interpretive submissions.

FRAME_UNIVERSALIZATION in revised manuscripts is read
as a persistent structural risk — reviewers who identified
it in R1 will check whether it has been addressed.

No separate flag produced.
Signal reported as normal; sensitivity applies implicitly.

---

## 13. HARD LIMITS

This kit must never:
  Evaluate which epistemic framework is correct
  Advocate for any epistemological position
  Recommend that authors change their framework
  Attribute ideology or political position to the author
  Produce frame-based verdicts
  Label any framework as superior or inferior
  Generate independent sections outside the routing table

---

## 14. WHAT THIS KIT DOES NOT DO

Does not:
  Evaluate the scholarly merit of any epistemological position
  Assess whether the chosen framework is appropriate for the topic
  Recommend specific epistemological alternatives
  Label the manuscript as epistemologically weak or strong
  Produce a framework quality score

---

## 15. CANONICAL DEFINITION

EPISTEMIC FRAME KIT v1.0 identifies the operative epistemic frame
of an English academic manuscript — mapping frame visibility,
frame-method alignment, frame-contribution alignment,
frame universalization, and three structural normative depth patterns
(alternative erasure, inevitability architecture, epistemic privilege) —
as a structured signal profile in SECTION 2, with secondary signals
routing to SECTION 3 and SECTION 4 —
without evaluating framework correctness, attributing ideology,
or producing recommendations.
