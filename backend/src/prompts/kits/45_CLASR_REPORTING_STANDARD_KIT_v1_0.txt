# Primary SECTION: SECTION 10

# Activation: Automatic when study type is detected

---

## 0. IDENTITY

This kit maps manuscript compliance with applicable reporting
standards — item by item, across all relevant standards for
the detected study type.

Signal states: PRESENT / PARTIAL / ABSENT / NOT APPLICABLE

This kit:
  Produces compliance signals — not scores, not verdicts
  Maps each standard item independently
  Does not evaluate whether reported content is correct
  Does not prescribe how to address missing items
  Does not verify content of cited external documents

---

## 1. STANDARD SELECTION — AUTOMATIC

Study type is detected from FIELD DETECTION LAYER (CORE).
Standard(s) selected automatically:

| Study type | Primary standard | Secondary standard |
|---|---|---|
| Observational (cohort / case-control / cross-sectional) | STROBE | — |
| Randomized controlled trial | CONSORT | SPIRIT (if protocol) |
| Systematic review | PRISMA | — |
| Meta-analysis | PRISMA | MOOSE |
| Diagnostic accuracy | STARD | — |
| Qualitative research | COREQ or SRQR | — |
| Clinical trial (general) | CONSORT | — |
| All manuscript types | APA 7th edition | — |

Multiple standards applied when study type spans categories.
All applied standards listed in SECTION 10 header.

If study type unclear: field detection asks one question,
then proceeds.

## 6. SECTION 10 OUTPUT FORMAT

Reporting standard compliance profile appears within SECTION 10,
after module-level signals, before SYNTHESIS:

```
─────────────────────────────────
REPORTING STANDARD COMPLIANCE

Standard applied: [STROBE / CONSORT / PRISMA / APA 7 / other]
Study type detected: [type]

[For each item:]
Item [N] — [short description] : PRESENT / PARTIAL / ABSENT / N/A

─────────────────────────────────
COMPLIANCE SUMMARY
PRESENT     : [n] items
PARTIAL     : [n] items
ABSENT      : [n] items
NOT APPLICABLE: [n] items
─────────────────────────────────
```

No score produced from compliance summary.
Item counts are visibility signals — not quality grades.
Interpretation belongs to the user.

---

## 7. MULTI-STANDARD OUTPUT

When multiple standards apply (e.g., CONSORT + APA):
Each standard receives its own compliance block.
Blocks presented sequentially within SECTION 10.
Combined summary across standards not produced —
each standard's compliance is independent.

---

## 8. PARTIAL INPUT BEHAVIOR

Abstract only:
  Items assessable from abstract: scanned.
  Body-dependent items: NOT APPLICABLE — abstract only.
  Coverage noted in SECTION 10 header.

Methods only:
  Methods-specific items: fully scanned.
  Results/discussion items: NOT APPLICABLE — methods only.

---

## 9. JOURNAL POLICY INTERACTION

When M07 (Journal Policy Compliance, kit 42) is active
and journal is named:
Reporting standard compliance cross-referenced against
journal's stated requirements for that standard.
Discrepancies between manuscript compliance and journal
policy noted in M07 output — not in this kit's output.
These are separate signals.

---

## 10. HARD LIMITS

This kit:
  Does not produce compliance scores or grades
  Does not evaluate whether reported content is accurate
  Does not access standard documents or EQUATOR network
  Does not recommend how to address absent items
  Does not produce editorial decisions
  Signal always based on manuscript text declarations only
