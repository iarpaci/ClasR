Common help topics

What can we help with?

Upload formats

Clasr accepts PDF and DOCX manuscripts. For pasted text, use the paste option on the new reading screen.

Reading modes

Author mode explains signals clearly, Reviewer mode is denser, and Editor mode adds academic context.

Q-profile

Q1 is strict, Q2 is balanced, Q3 is more flexible, and Auto estimates the best calibration.

Signal reports

A report shows manuscript behavior patterns. It is not a score, verdict, or acceptance prediction.

Exports

Completed readings can be downloaded as PDF, DOCX, or TXT from the report page.

History

If history is off, Clasr will not keep completed readings. Turn it on from Account settings.

Simple questions

Quick answers

Why does my report say “high risk”?

It means several manuscript behaviors need attention, such as claim drift, unclear methods, inconsistency, or weak evidence alignment.

Can Clasr rewrite my manuscript?

No. Clasr makes behavioral signals visible. It does not rewrite, score, or decide for you.

Where do I change default reading mode?

Go to Account settings and update Reading defaults.

Why can’t I see reading history?

History may be turned off. Open Readings to see the status, or turn history on in Account settings.
