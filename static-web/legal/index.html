Clasr Legal

Policies, agreements, and data practices

Where to find what Clasr does with your data, what governs your use of the Service, and where Clasr's compliance program currently stands.

Core commitments

Your manuscript remains yours. You keep ownership of uploaded manuscripts, and Clasr processes them only to provide the requested reading and analysis features.

No model training or permanent manuscript storage. Manuscript content is not used to train AI models and is not permanently stored by Clasr after processing.

No publication verdicts. Reports identify signals, limits, and uncertainties; they do not provide peer review, editorial decisions, or acceptance predictions.

  • You must have the right to upload any manuscript you submit.
  • You may not upload confidential, restricted, or third-party manuscripts unless you are authorized to do so.
  • Signal reports are one input for human readers; academic, editorial, submission, and revision decisions remain with people.
  • Payment, account, cookie, security, and sub-processor details are explained in the sections below.
