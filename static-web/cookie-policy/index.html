Cookie Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Clasr uses cookies and similar technologies on clasr.ai and within the Service, and the choices available to you.

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device and store some information about your preferences or past actions. "Similar technologies" refers to other mechanisms that work in a comparable way, such as local storage.

2. Cookies We Use

Clasr currently uses only essential cookies and browser local storage. No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies are in use.

CategoryPurposeCan Be Disabled?
Essential Required for the Service to function: maintaining your login session and remembering your plan and settings. Stored in browser local storage. No — disabling these prevents login and core Service features.
Preference Remembering choices you make, such as your preferred output mode or display setting. Yes, though some features may default to standard settings if disabled.
Analytics Understanding how visitors use clasr.ai to improve the Service. Not currently deployed. Will require consent before activation.

3. Cookies Set by Others

No third-party payment cookies are currently used. This Policy will be updated before checkout is enabled to identify the payment provider and any strictly necessary checkout cookies.

4. Your Choices

Because Clasr currently uses only essential cookies and local storage, no consent prompt is required at this time. Before any non-essential cookies or tracking technologies are introduced — including analytics — Clasr will implement a consent mechanism that allows you to choose which categories you accept. You will also be able to update your preference at any time.

You can always control or clear cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies will prevent login and core Service features from working.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated Policy at clasr.ai/legal with a revised effective date.

6. Contact Us

Questions about this Policy can be directed to legal@clasr.ai.
