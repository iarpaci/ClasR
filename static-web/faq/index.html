FAQ

Common Questions

Direct answers. Anything not covered here, email hello@clasr.ai.

Is Clasr a peer review tool?

No. It maps how a manuscript behaves, not whether it should be accepted.

Does Clasr score manuscripts?

No score, no rating, no acceptance probability. Just a severity and an inference type per signal, both descriptive, not evaluative.

Will Clasr rewrite my manuscript?

No. It surfaces signals, you make the revision. Once AI rewrites your prose, it stops being your manuscript.

What does Clasr explicitly not do?

Verify proofs, check plagiarism, recommend journals, replace peer review, or predict outcomes. All on purpose.

What languages does Clasr support?

English only for now. We're working on adding more.

What kinds of manuscripts does Clasr handle?

Quantitative, empirical, structured academic work, across health, law, social science, and tech. Theoretical work too, with some limits. Essays and literary texts, not really.

What happens to my manuscript after processing?

Deleted. Nothing trains AI models. Reports stay in your account 7 days by default, configurable down to 24 hours or auto-delete on download.

Is Clasr available where I am?

Yes, everywhere, including Türkiye.

How much does Clasr cost?

Trial: $25, one reading. Researcher: $59/month, 5 readings. Professional: $119/month, 12 readings. Extra readings: $29 each. Institutional pricing is custom.

Are there other Clasr tools?

Yes. Clasr Suite adds argument architecture, citation function, figure integrity, reproducibility audits, and publication strategy, enterprise only for now.

How is Clasr different from peer review simulators?

Those score you like a reviewer would. Clasr shows the pattern, skips the verdict.

How is Clasr different from literature discovery tools?

Different stage entirely. Those help you find papers. Clasr reads the one you've already written.

Can institutions use Clasr across departments or journals?

Yes, through Custom and Enterprise contracts, priced per-seat, per-manuscript, or custom depending on the setup. Contact Enterprise.

Can funding agencies use Clasr?

Yes. It tracks the things most funders already check, like data availability, code sharing, pre-registration, ethics, and funding transparency, across an entire portfolio of papers.

Where can I find more detail?

The Signal Architecture page covers the modular breakdown, and How It Works covers the pipeline. Anything else, email hello@clasr.ai.
